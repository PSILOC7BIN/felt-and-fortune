# Duolingo Shop, Avatar/Outfit Cosmetics, and Quests (2019–2026, focus 2024–2026)

Research date: 2026-11-24. Official Duolingo sources are the blog, shareholder letters on SEC EDGAR, and the Duolingo Reddit statement quoted by press. Fan, guide and blog sites (duoplanet, duolingoguides, happilyevertravels, lingoly, the fandom wiki) are marked **[third-party]**. Their prices and mechanics change often and vary by platform and A/B test bucket.

## 1. Shop layout and items (gem prices, sections, avatars)

### Takeaway
I found no official, current Duolingo price list. Third-party guides from 2024–2026 put prices at roughly 200 gems for a Streak Freeze, about 350 gems for a heart refill, about 450 gems for a Timer Boost, and 50 gems for Double or Nothing. As of 2025–2026, the main purchasable items are streak protection, hearts and energy, and boosts. Cosmetics have moved from a gem-bought Duo outfit shelf (removed in 2022) to event-earned avatar suits (2026).

### Cited Findings
- **2021–2022 shop sections and items** **[third-party]**, from an article published Jun 2021 and updated Aug 2022: Streak Freeze, Weekend Amulet (Android and desktop only), Double or Nothing (a 50-gem wager), Heart Refill, Gems bought with real money, Outfits, and Bonus Skills (some languages only). The shop opened from a bottom-right shop icon on mobile and a Shop tab on desktop. — [duoplanet, "We Need To Talk About The Duolingo Shop"](https://duoplanet.com/we-need-to-talk-about-the-duolingo-shop/)
- **Real-money gem packs, UK, about 2021–2022** **[third-party]**: 1,200 gems for £4.99, 3,000 for £9.99, 6,500 for £19.99. — [duoplanet](https://duoplanet.com/we-need-to-talk-about-the-duolingo-shop/)
- **Streak Freeze** **[third-party, Jan 2026]**: about 200 gems on mobile (10 lingots on web historically). Each freeze covers one missed day. — [lingoly.io, Streak Freeze (Jan 2026)](https://lingoly.io/duolingo-streak-freeze/)
- **Heart/Energy refill** **[third-party]**: 350 gems in the shop, 450 if bought mid-lesson. Double or Nothing costs 50 gems and Animated Status 500 gems. — [duolingoguides Gems Calculator](https://duolingoguides.com/?p=7381); [happilyevertravels](https://happilyevertravels.com/?p=6333)
- **Timer Boost** **[third-party]**: reported at 450 gems, offered when you run out of time in timed challenges such as Match Madness. The author warns that prices change. — [happilyevertravels](https://happilyevertravels.com/?p=6333)
- **The shop is thin by late 2025** **[third-party, reader comment, Dec 2025]**: a commenter said outfits were gone and the shop had "only streak freezes to buy." This is anecdotal. — [duoplanet comments](https://duoplanet.com/we-need-to-talk-about-the-duolingo-shop/)
- **Avatar builder (official, 2023-07-19)**: on the profile page, users can customize hairstyle, body shape, piercings, skin tone and accessories such as glasses. Once you switch to an avatar you can no longer upload a photo. The post does not mention any paid items. — [Duolingo Blog, Avatar creator](https://blog.duolingo.com/avatar-creator/)
- **Avatar customizations named as a monetization lever (official, Q4 FY2025 letter, 2026-02-26)**: the letter lists "selling avatar customizations and our own native ad format" as monetization not at odds with DAU growth. — [Q4 2025 shareholder letter (IR)](https://investors.duolingo.com/static-files/961ce633-3cee-49d0-bd7a-2c63731d45fb); [Customer Experience Dive summary](https://www.customerexperiencedive.com/news/duolingo-limit-friction-growth-slows/813414/)
- **Avatar Collections system (official, Q3 FY2026 letter, 2026-11-03)**:
  - Junior's Toy Hunt was "the first event built around our new Avatar Collections system," in which learners "collect puzzle pieces to unlock avatar outfits."
  - It was "one of our biggest experiment wins for getting learners to complete more lessons."
  - Management says "collection events can be a meaningful opportunity to grow in-app purchases over time" and plans "a regular cadence of collection events."
  - The CEO's letter names "Scraps" as the grand-prize outfit.
  - [Q3 2026 shareholder letter, SEC EDGAR](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001562088/000162828026069474/q3fy26duolingo9-30x26share.htm)
- **Avatar Suits (official blog, 2026-10-19)**: "During limited-time events, you can make progress to collect special suits for your Duolingo Avatar." Each event has a window to earn the featured suits. "Once you've collected a suit, it's yours, and you can cycle through the suits you've earned." The post does not mention gem or cash prices. — [Duolingo Blog, October 2026 update](https://blog.duolingo.com/october-2026-update/)
- **Event examples and backlash (press, 2026-11-19)**:
  - Current events include Duo's Pokémon Party (a Pokémon GO collaboration that gives avatar suits plus Pokémon GO credits) and Lily's Fright Fest, a puzzle-piece event that ran past Halloween.
  - Several events run at once. After each lesson, learners click through event screens and treasure chests that can take several taps.
  - Users complained. One said they now log in only weekly.
  - A Duolingo representative posted on Reddit: "Your thumbs deserve a break and we know you'd like to focus more on learning." Duolingo is testing an opt-out from avatar-suit events and some post-lesson screens, starting with a small group, with a wider rollout planned for December 2026.
  - [Android Authority, 2026-11-19](https://androidauthority.com/duolingo-event-screen-opt-out-3737299)

### Inferences
- Duolingo's 2026 cosmetic model is "earn by learning, with optional purchase to accelerate." Collection events earn puzzle pieces through lessons, and management ties them explicitly to IAP growth. The exact way pieces or suits can be bought (gems or cash) is not public in the sources I found.
- The Nov 2026 backlash is a direct design warning for a blackjack app: post-session reward screens and stacked concurrent events create friction. Duolingo is adding an opt-out.

### Gaps
- I found no official current gem price list, and no verified 2024–2026 prices for XP Boost, Legendary, or real-money gem packs in USD. The fandom wiki Gem page returned HTTP 402.
- No source shows how the shop is currently organized into sections or featured slots, or whether suit pieces can be bought with gems or cash.
- I found no source for "paid avatar suits" being sold directly in 2025. The 2025 letters only flagged avatar customization as a plan, and 2026 is the first evidence of it shipping.

## 2. Duo costume / outfit history and whether they sold

### Takeaway
From about 2019 to 2022, gem-bought outfits for Duo the owl were sold: Formal Attire, Champagne Tracksuit, and Super Duo. They were removed during 2022 and no sales data was ever disclosed. I found no "Champion Jersey" outfit in any source.

### Cited Findings
- **Official promo, 2019-08-23**: Duolingo tweeted three "owlfits": "fancy formal attire, a snazzy champagne tracksuit, or a bold superhero costume." — [Duolingo on X](https://x.com/duolingo/status/1164913518656786433?lang=en)
- **Prices and removal** **[third-party, fandom wiki via search snippet]**: Formal Attire cost 400 gems, Luxury/Champagne Tracksuit 600, and Super Duo 1,000. Removal reportedly started with iOS in January 2022 and finished for all users by May–June 2022. — [Duolingo Wiki, Outfits](https://duolingo.fandom.com/wiki/Outfits)
- **Reader comments about removal** **[anecdotal]**: in June 2022 a reader said outfits had vanished from Android with no gem refund. In November 2023 another said outfits and bonus skills were gone. — [duoplanet comments](https://duoplanet.com/we-need-to-talk-about-the-duolingo-shop/)

### Inferences
- The removal, with no replacement for about 4 years, suggests the outfits were not a meaningful revenue or engagement driver. This is an inference, not a disclosed fact. Duo outfits were visible only to the owner, unlike avatar suits, which show on a social profile.

### Gaps
- I found no sales, attach-rate, or revenue data for Duo outfits. The "Champion Jersey" item was not found and may be confused with a merch item or another app.

## 3. Quests tab: daily, Friends Quests, monthly challenges, rewards, impact

### Takeaway
Daily quests are 3 per day with tiered chests. Friends Quests are a weekly co-op challenge with a random friend and run 5 days. Monthly challenges award a badge for accumulating quest points. The only official impact number I found is a 2022 Duolingo statement that learners who follow friends are 5.6x more likely to finish their course. That stat is correlational and about friends, not quests specifically.

### Cited Findings
- **Friends Quests (official blog, 2022-09-09)**:
  - **Pairing:** each week you are randomly paired with a mutual-follow friend after 1 p.m. ET Tuesday, if you completed at least one lesson that week. The challenge is also random, such as an XP target or a number of perfect lessons.
  - **Duration and reward:** you have 5 days. The reward is a treasure chest with gems and a 2x XP bonus.
  - **Social features:** you can send preset nudges and gift your partner an XP boost bought with gems.
  - **Monthly Goal link:** Friends Quests give more "Quest Points" toward the Monthly Goal badge than daily challenges.
  - **Availability:** mobile only, with an opt-out in settings.
  - **Impact stat:** "learners who follow friends are 5.6x more likely to finish their language course."
  - [Duolingo Blog, Friends Quests](https://blog.duolingo.com/friends-quests/)
- **Daily quests** **[third-party, 2023–2025]**: 3 new quests every 24 hours, such as earning XP, completing lessons, or getting perfect scores. Each gives 1 quest point toward the monthly challenge. — [duolingoguides Quests Guide](https://duolingoguides.com/what-is-a-quest-in-duolingo/); [Cherish Study (2024)](https://cherishstudy.com/duolingo-daily-quests/)
- **Monthly challenge varies by platform** **[third-party]**: one guide says Android may still show a 1000-XP monthly challenge while iOS shows quest-based goals, which suggests A/B testing. A completed Friends Quest reportedly counts as 5 quests toward the monthly badge. — [duolingoguides](https://duolingoguides.com/what-is-a-quest-in-duolingo/)
- **Weekend Quest** **[third-party, Jan 2026, unconfirmed by Duolingo]**: a guide reports a new Weekend Quest alongside daily and friend quests. — [lingoly.io Weekend Quest](https://lingoly.io/duolingo-weekend-quest/)
- **Analyst view** **[third-party, 2025-04-14]**: Deconstructor of Fun says Daily and Monthly Quests are designed for longer sessions and daily retention, giving the satisfaction of completing something "today." Its related streak analysis reports that users with at least one Friend Streak are 22% more likely to complete their daily lesson. This is a reported figure, not a quest-specific controlled result. — [Deconstructor of Fun](https://www.deconstructoroffun.com/blog/2025/4/14/duolingo-how-the-15b-app-uses-gaming-principles-to-supercharge-dau-growth); [DoF streaks mechanic](https://duolingo.deconstructoroffun.com/mechanics/streaks)

### Inferences
- The structure stacks daily, weekly social, and monthly collection goals, so each tier feeds the next through quest points. This maps cleanly to a blackjack app: daily drills, a weekly co-op with a friend, and a monthly badge.

### Gaps
- I found no official Duolingo number on quests' effect on DAU or retention, and no official blog post on the quests tab itself.
- Official refresh time (local midnight?) and a "Quest streak" mechanic were not confirmed by any source.

## 4. Reward chests: contents, randomization, disclosure

### Takeaway
Third-party sources describe chest contents in approximate terms ("usually," "about"): small gem amounts and XP boosts. That suggests some variability, but Duolingo publishes no odds or contents disclosure that I could find.

### Cited Findings
- **Chest types and contents** **[third-party, duoplanet, updated 2024-03-18]**:
  - **Daily quest chests:** up to 3 per day. Bronze holds about 5 gems, silver about 10, and gold about 15, and gold may include an XP Boost.
  - **Friends Quest chest:** 100 gems plus a 30-minute XP Boost, once per week.
  - **Early Bird / Night Owl chests:** always a 15-minute XP Boost, mobile only. A Dec 2024 reader comment says these were removed.
  - **Path chests:** unlock on the learning path. Some are video chests, and level-completion chests usually give a 15-minute XP Boost.
  - **Share bonus:** a gem chest is sometimes offered for sharing progress.
  - The only element described as explicitly random is Friends Quest pairing and challenge selection.
  - [duoplanet, Duolingo Chests](https://duoplanet.com/duolingo-chests/)
- **Conflicting tiers** **[third-party]**: an older source describes "Common/Rare" chests. Others describe bronze chests at 3–5 gems and gold chests with about 15 gems or a 15-minute XP Boost. Tiers have evidently changed over time. — [happilyevertravels Daily Quests](https://happilyevertravels.com/duolingo-daily-quests/); [duolingoguides](https://duolingoguides.com/what-is-a-quest-in-duolingo/)
- **Multi-tap chests in 2026 events (press, 2026-11-19)**: treasure chests in post-lesson flows may need several taps before they give rewards, and users cite this as friction. — [Android Authority](https://androidauthority.com/duolingo-event-screen-opt-out-3737299)

### Inferences
- The chests are earned rather than bought, which keeps Duolingo clear of loot-box disclosure rules. I found no paid random-reward items. A blackjack app wanting to avoid gambling-adjacent optics should keep chest contents fixed or disclosed, and never sell randomized boxes for cash. This is a design inference, not a legal finding.

### Gaps
- I found no official Duolingo disclosure of chest odds or contents, and no Duolingo statement on whether chest rewards are randomized.

## 5. Cosmetics as a revenue line and IAP trends

### Takeaway
IAP is a small and shrinking line. It fell from about 4% of revenue in 2025 to about 2.2% in Q3 2026, and was down 38% YoY to $6.9M that quarter. Duolingo nonetheless names avatar collection events as its path to growing IAP "over time."

### Cited Findings
- **IAP revenue by quarter (official, SEC 8-K shareholder letters, $ thousands):**

  | Quarter | IAP revenue | Prior-year quarter | YoY | Share of revenue |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Q1 2026 | 8,446 | 9,442 (Q1 2025) | −11% | ~2.9% (vs ~4.1%) |
  | Q2 2026 | 8,002 | 10,390 (Q2 2025) | −23% | ~2.7% (vs ~4.1%) |
  | Q3 2026 | 6,880 | 11,096 (Q3 2025) | −38% | ~2.2% of $309.0M revenue (vs ~4.1%) |

  Share-of-revenue percentages are my own calculations from the reported tables.
  - Sources: [Q1 2026](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001562088/000162828026029790/q1fy26duolingo3-31x26share.htm); [Q2 2026](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001562088/000162828026053299/q2fy26duolingo6-30x26share.htm); [Q3 2026](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001562088/000162828026069474/q3fy26duolingo9-30x26share.htm)
- **Definition (official)**: bookings include "in-app purchases of virtual goods ("IAPs")." — [Q1 2026 letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001562088/000162828026029790/q1fy26duolingo3-31x26share.htm)
- **Strategy statements (official)**:
  - **Q2 2026:** Duolingo is "working on ways to monetize that are not at odds with user growth" and focusing on "increase revenue without adding friction to free users." — [Q2 2026 letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001562088/000162828026053299/q2fy26duolingo6-30x26share.htm)
  - **Q3 2026:** Duolingo is "gradually leaning more into monetization in ways that preserve the quality of our free experience." Collection events "can be a meaningful opportunity to grow in-app purchases over time." Q4 guidance cites "continued strength in advertising and in-app purchases." — [Q3 2026 letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001562088/000162828026069474/q3fy26duolingo9-30x26share.htm)
  - **Q4 2025 (2026-02-26):** avatar customizations are named as a non-DAU-conflicting monetization option. — [Customer Experience Dive](https://www.customerexperiencedive.com/news/duolingo-limit-friction-growth-slows/813414/)
- **Context (official, Q3 2026)**: 64.9M DAU (+29% YoY), 13.1M paid subscribers, revenue $309.0M (+14%). Subscription revenue was $270.0M (+18%) and dominates. — [Q3 2026 letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001562088/000162828026069474/q3fy26duolingo9-30x26share.htm)

### Inferences
- The IAP decline through 2026 likely reflects Super/Max subscribers getting hearts and other perks without buying gems, plus the friction-reduction push. This is an inference, since the letters give no reason for the drop. Cosmetics were not yet a material line as of Q3 2026, and their value to Duolingo so far is engagement (more lessons completed), with revenue only hoped for.

### Gaps
- I did not retrieve Q4 2025 or full-year 2025 IAP figures directly.
- Duolingo does not break out cosmetic or avatar revenue separately, and gives no conversion or ARPU data for collection events.
