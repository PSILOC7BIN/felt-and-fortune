# Game Cosmetics: What Sells in Card, Casino-Themed, Chess and Casual Games, and How It Is Priced, Packaged and Rotated

Research date: 2026-11-24. Scope: evidence for designing a cosmetic catalog (card backs, table felts, coach/mascot skins, avatar items) for an 18+ blackjack education app with an earned currency. Each claim carries the date of its source. Prices are USD unless noted and change over time, so treat them as snapshots.

## 1. Card backs and table/board themes (Hearthstone, Microsoft Solitaire Collection, solitaire apps, poker apps, Balatro, Chess.com)

### Takeaway
In card games, card backs are the standard cosmetic. They are earned at least as often as they are sold: through ranked seasons, achievements, events, pre-order bundles and free collabs. Paid card backs are cheap, about $1–3 each or $3 for a bundle. Table and board themes are usually free personalization or a perk of a premium subscription, not stand-alone items. In social poker apps the visible cosmetics are avatars and emotes, tied to earned currencies.

### Cited Findings
**Hearthstone (Blizzard)**
- Card back sources (wiki, current): Basic (everyone has it), Fireside Gatherings, Achievements, Solo Adventures, Rewards Track milestones, and hero-skin purchases or expansion pre-order bundles. Some backs are always obtainable and others only for a limited time, and once you own a back you keep it. — [Hearthstone Wiki: card back](https://hearthstone.wiki.gg/wiki/card_back)
- Seasonal (Ranked) card backs come from winning Ranked games each month. A separate Legend card back is given for reaching Legend rank. There are also Esports backs (from taking part in events), "Games" backs that promote other Blizzard titles, platform "Promotion" backs, and pre-purchase backs. — [Hearthstone Wiki: card back](https://hearthstone.wiki.gg/wiki/card_back)
- Older seasonal rule: win 5 Ranked games in the month to get that month's back. — [GameSkinny guide](https://www.gameskinny.com/tips/hearthstone-card-backs-guide-a-comprehensive-list-and-how-to-unlock-them/) (undated, older)
- Since 2021 (patch 20.4), most card backs more than a year old can be bought for gold, at 500 or 600 gold depending on rarity. 94 card backs and 11 hero skins were made purchasable for gold at that point, and all Ranked card backs were later made purchasable in the collection manager. — [Out of Games, 2021](https://outof.games/hearthstone/3210-94-card-backs-and-11-hero-skins-in-hearthstone-will-be-purchasable-for-gold-in-patch-204-this-week); [Hearthstone Wiki](https://hearthstone.wiki.gg/wiki/card_back)
- Shop example (2021): the Thrall card back cost 500 Gold or $3, so the same back had both an earned-currency price and a real-money price. — [Out of Games](https://outof.games/news/2699-the-thunder-king-shaman-hero-stolen-thunder-card-back-and-thrall-card-back-are-now-available-in-the-hearthstone-shop/)
- Player backlash: a forum thread objected to a formerly exclusive Ranked back (Naxxramas) being put up for sale. Making earned prestige items buyable can annoy players. — [Blizzard forums](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/hearthstone/t/why-is-an-exclusive-ranked-card-back-for-sale-now-naxxramas/37716)
- Hearthstone's Tavern Pass gives several free rewards for every premium one, the reverse of most passes. — [Deconstructor of Fun, Jun 2022](https://deconstructoroffun.com/blog/2022/6/4/battle-passes-analysis)

A 2025 Smurfs crossover added a joint collection album "packed with exclusive card sets", and a Google Play event promoted a time-limited "Grand Library Album" that players could complete for a prize. — [Senal News, 2025](https://senalnews.com/en/content/the-smurfs-make-their-way-to-solitaire-grand-harvest-cards-game); [Google Play event](https://play.google.com/store/apps/eventdetails/4829386363150108212)

**Chess.com**

- Premium plans list perks such as lessons, puzzle explanations, Opening Explorer and Custom Flair (a cosmetic profile badge). - An old forum answer says premium members can save 5 custom themes and free members 1. This may be out of date. — [Chess.com forum](https://www.chess.com/forum/view/help-support/what-does-it-mean-by-5-themes)

### Inferences
- In card, board and puzzle games, the most common merchandising model has three parts: (a) free base themes; (b) prestige card backs earned through rank, events or achievements; (c) cheap paid backs or bundles at $1–3, or backs folded into a subscription. Big-ticket card-back sales are rare.
- Hearthstone's 500/600-gold catalog of older backs shows a workable earned-currency pattern: a back starts as a time-limited prestige reward, then returns later at a fixed earned-currency price. Rank-based or "Legend" items should stay exclusive, or players resent the change.
- Balatro and Chess.com show that themed and collab decks are effective engagement and PR tools even when given away free. For an education app, free themed decks tied to lesson milestones fit this pattern.
- For a blackjack app, combined "table sets" (felt, card back, chips) look like a gap: poker players build their own today through mods and third-party sellers.

### Gaps
- No current (2024–2026) gold price list for Hearthstone card backs, and no usage data on which backs are most popular. Blizzard publishes none.
- No official per-item list of Chess.com board and piece sets that are premium-only.
- No in-app price lists for WSOP, Zynga Poker or Solitaire Grand Harvest cosmetics (they are visible only inside the apps).
- No revenue split by cosmetic type for any card or casino game.

## 2. Character/mascot skins (Clash Royale, Brawl Stars, Fortnite, Among Us): which categories earn the most

### Takeaway
Character skins are the anchor cosmetic and carry the highest prices. Emotes, banners and other add-ons are priced lower and used as fillers in passes and bundles. Fortnite's daily-rotating shop with rarity-based prices is the reference model. Supercell puts its tower skin and emote in the paid pass, which turns cosmetics into a reason to buy the pass. No public source gives a revenue breakdown by category (skins vs emotes vs boards).

### Cited Findings
**Fortnite**
- The Item Shop resets daily at 00:00 UTC. — [VideoGamer](https://www.videogamer.com/guides/fortnite-item-shop-today/)
- Price ladder (2023): Legendary outfits 2,000 V-Bucks, Epic 1,500, Rare 1,200, Uncommon 800. Emotes, gliders and pickaxes are generally 500–800. — [Sportskeeda, 2023](https://www.sportskeeda.com/esports/latest-items-features-added-fortnite-item-shop-updated-v-bucks-prices). A 2026 guide says most outfits cost 1,200–2,000 V-Bucks depending on rarity and detail. — [DesignBeep, Jun 2026](https://designbeep.com/2026/06/12/fortnite-item-shop/)
- In 2024 shop listings, an emote cost 800 V-Bucks. By early 2023 the full catalog of Item Shop cosmetics totaled about 2.4M V-Bucks, with about 700 emotes worth about 176,000 V-Bucks. — [AFK Gaming, Apr 2024](https://afkgaming.com/esports/guide/fortnite-item-shop-9-apr-2024-skins-emotes-gliders-wraps-melee-more); [Sportskeeda](https://www.sportskeeda.com/fortnite/how-much-cost-buy-every-single-fortnite-cosmetic-item-item-shop)
- Currency packs (2023): 5,000 V-Bucks for $31.99 and 13,000 for $79.99, so the Legendary tier works out to about $13–16 per skin. — [Sportskeeda](https://www.sportskeeda.com/esports/latest-items-features-added-fortnite-item-shop-updated-v-bucks-prices)
- Fortnite earned an estimated $5.8B in 2021 (Business of Apps estimate). That figure includes the Battle Pass, so it is not cosmetics alone. Hard-to-earn Battle Pass skins carry a "Legendary" label, and Season 1 skins act as status symbols because they can no longer be obtained. — [Naavik Part 1, Jun 2022](https://naavik.co/deep-dives/selling-cosmetics-part1/); [Naavik Part 2, Jul 2022](https://naavik.co/f2p-mobile/selling-cosmetics-part-2/)

**Clash Royale (Supercell)**
- The Pass Royale restructure (season starting April 3; the post appears to be 2024–2025) created three tiers. Free: 1 Gold Crate, 35 Crown Chests, 2,500 Season Tokens. Gold Pass $5.99: wild cards, crates, chest keys, perks, plus 1 Tower Skin and 1 Emote. Diamond Pass $11.99: adds 500 gems, a Champion card and an Animated Battle Banner. Gold-to-Diamond upgrade: $6.99. The pass price had not changed since 2019 (then $4.99). — [Supercell blog](https://supercell.com/en/games/clashroyale/blog/news/pass-royale-price-changes-2/)
- Each season's pass has its own exclusive tower skin and emote (e.g. Season 17's Ghostly Treasure skin, Season 27's Kitchen Tower skin). They can be earned only during that season and are kept permanently once unlocked. — [GINX](https://www.ginx.tv/en/clash-royale-season-17-pass-royale-rewards); [Supercell Season 27](https://supercell.com/en/games/clashroyale/blog/news/season-27-breakdown-rewards-2)
- December 2025 update: duplicate max-level cards now convert into Crystals, which can be spent on emotes. This creates a currency sink for cosmetics. — [Supercell, Dec 2025](https://supercell.com/en/games/clashroyale/blog/release-notes/december-update-2025)
- Deconstructor of Fun notes that the pass at first reduced Clash Royale's revenue until spending depth and progression pressure were added, while the same feature raised Clash of Clans' revenue. — [Deconstructor of Fun, Jun 2022](https://deconstructoroffun.com/blog/2022/6/4/battle-passes-analysis)

**Brawl Stars (Supercell)**
- Skin rarity price ladder in gems: Rare 29, Super Rare 79, Epic 149, Mythic 199, Legendary 299. **Category revenue evidence (indirect)**
- In 2021, gun skins made up 53% of all CS:GO-related purchases (DMarket). — [Naavik Part 1](https://naavik.co/deep-dives/selling-cosmetics-part1/)
- PUBG Mobile skins, accessories and emotes are mostly gated behind events, passes and gachas (GameRefinery). — [WN Hub](https://wnhub.io/news/finance/item-44845)
- Liquid & Grit report headline: "Adding Progressions to Skins Boosts Revenue 25%". It covers Mobile Legends hero-skin collections, and the underlying data is paywalled. — [Liquid & Grit market coverage](https://www.liquidandgrit.com/market-coverage/page/3/)

### Inferences
- Skins on the main character or object (outfit, hero, tower) sit at the top of the price ladder, about 2–10x the price of emotes and banners. Rarity labels map directly to price. For this app, coach/mascot skins and full table sets fit the "outfit" tier, and card backs, emotes and chip styles fit the lower "accessory" tier.
- Exclusives tied to a season pass (one hero skin plus one emote) are Supercell's main pass-selling lever.
- Among Us shows that a theme bundle can be sold for earned currency, with licensed or premium themes held back for the paid currency. That two-currency split can be adapted to an earned-only economy by gating the top tiers on effort (quests, streaks) instead of money.

### Gaps
- No public, credible revenue split of skins vs emotes vs boards/backgrounds for any of these games. Supercell, Epic and Innersloth do not publish it.
- No sell-through data per Fortnite shop item (who buys what, and how often items return).

## 3. Season/battle passes: free vs premium tracks, tiers, prices, completion and conversion, quest engagement

### Takeaway
Mobile passes usually run about a month and cost $5–15 for the basic premium tier, often with a $10–13 upper tier. Their rewards are pitched at about 10–20x the price in value. They convert well but have limited ARPPU. In top games they account for 1–40% of revenue (Google, 2021). Pass buyers play more sessions and return more regularly. No authoritative public data on completion rates was found.

### Cited Findings
- Most mobile battle passes cost $5–15 in the basic tier. Passes have "strong conversion but very limited ARPPU potential", rewards are typically worth 10–20x the price, and the standard mobile length is about one month (TFT runs three months). Wild Rift has 50 standard levels plus 25 premium-only levels. Supercell sells Gold Pass bundles at a 20% discount on its web store. Archero has two premium tiers, one for low spenders and one for high spenders. — [Deconstructor of Fun, Jun 2022](https://deconstructoroffun.com/blog/2022/6/4/battle-passes-analysis)
- Track design patterns: both tracks progress at the same pace and differ in reward type (Royal Match, Wild Rift); the premium track has extra tiers (COD Mobile, Beatstar); or the free track has more rewards (Hearthstone Tavern Pass). Recommendations: shorter passes, looping rewards after the last tier for completionists, and A/B tests to catch cannibalization of other purchases. — [Deconstructor of Fun, Jun 2022](https://deconstructoroffun.com/blog/2022/6/4/battle-passes-analysis)
- Premium pass purchases drive 1–40% of revenue in top games, and passes built into the core loop earn more than one-off event passes. Buyers play more sessions, play slightly longer, and have a higher DAU/MAU (Google internal data, 2020). Lower prices convert more players. Purchases held steady across the season. About 30 of the top 100 Google Play games had a pass. State of Survival merged pass quests into its daily quests. Persistent VIP perks for buyers (e.g. 10% off) encourage repeat purchases. — [Google Play Apps & Games, Sep 2021](https://medium.com/googleplaydev/how-battle-passes-can-boost-engagement-and-monetization-in-your-game-d296dee6ddf8)
- GameRefinery: 51% of US top-100 grossing games had a battle pass. In match-3 games, seasons last about 7–30+ days, the first stage is free in all games reviewed, and the free track rewards every stage or every 2–3 stages. Premium rewards are "several times more valuable" and include avatar frames and a completion super-prize. About 2/3 of games use points-for-levels progression. Time-limited 2x point events (e.g. 12 hours) help players who are stuck. — [GameRefinery guest post, Jun 2021](https://www.gamerefinery.com/7-tips-to-create-and-improve-battle-pass/)
- Appfigures (2025): cosmetic or unlock purchases appear in about 70% of top games, and about half have a battle pass. These are adoption rates, not revenue. — [Appfigures, May 2025](https://appfigures.com/resources/this-week-in-apps/20250516)
- Current price points: Clash Royale Gold $5.99 / Diamond $11.99. Brawl Pass $8.99 / Plus $12.99. — [Supercell CR](https://supercell.com/en/games/clashroyale/blog/news/pass-royale-price-changes-2/); [Supercell BS](https://supercell.com/en/games/brawlstars/blog/news/new-power-brawl-pass-changes-and-a-new-starr-drop-2)
- Completion rate: aggregator blogs widely cite "about 38% of buyers complete all tiers" (attributed to Unity or GameAnalytics), but their numbers conflict, e.g. 38.5% vs 41.2%, and no primary report was found. This figure is unverified. — [VoxBooster (aggregator)](https://voxbooster.com/blog/battle-pass-statistics-2026/)

### Inferences
- For an earned-currency app, the pass mechanics (time-boxed season, free vs premium tracks, daily and weekly quests feeding pass XP) can be kept without real money. The "premium" track could be unlocked by effort (e.g. finishing a course module) or by spending earned currency. A month-long season with about 30–50 tiers and looping rewards after the last tier matches the evidence.
- Tying pass progression to daily and weekly learning quests (as State of Survival merged its quests) is the main engagement lever in the evidence. Catch-up mechanics such as 2x XP windows help players who fall behind.

### Gaps
- No verified primary data on completion or conversion rates for battle passes. Specific benchmarks such as "X% of DAU buy the pass" are not public.
- No 2024–2026 analyst update (Sensor Tower, Liquid & Grit) quantifying pass revenue share. The Sensor Tower State of Mobile Gaming 2025 headline gives only total 2024 IAP of about $82B. — [Sensor Tower](https://sensortower.com/blog/state-of-mobile-gaming-2025)

## 4. Pricing tiers, rarity ladders, sets/collections, bundles, limited-time items, and what motivates purchase

### Takeaway
Rarity ladders of 4–5 steps that map to fixed prices are standard (Fortnite, Brawl Stars, Hearthstone's 500/600 gold). Scarcity comes from daily or weekly shop rotation, season-exclusive pass items, and themed or collab sets. Players' stated motives are self-expression, differentiation, status, aesthetics and social influence. Players are about 3x more likely to consider buying cosmetics than power.

### Cited Findings
- Rarity-to-price ladders: Fortnite Uncommon 800 / Rare 1,200 / Epic 1,500 / Legendary 2,000 V-Bucks (2023). Brawl Stars Rare 29 / Super Rare 79 / Epic 149 / Mythic 199 / Legendary 299 gems. Hearthstone older card backs 500 or 600 gold by rarity (2021). — [Sportskeeda](https://www.sportskeeda.com/esports/latest-items-features-added-fortnite-item-shop-updated-v-bucks-prices); [Mobile Matters](https://mobilematters.gg/brawl-stars/brawl-stars-guide-to-skin-rarities); [Out of Games](https://outof.games/hearthstone/3210-94-card-backs-and-11-hero-skins-in-hearthstone-will-be-purchasable-for-gold-in-patch-204-this-week)
- Bundles and sets: MSC sells themed card-back bundles (Kittens, Puppies, Retro) at $2.99, versus $0.99–1.99 for single backs. Fortnite sells sets (e.g. a 2,000 V-Bucks set in 2026 listings). Among Us Cosmicubes bundle about 70 items around one theme. — [App Store MSC](https://apps.apple.com/app/1103438575); [ProGameGuides, Mar 2026](https://progameguides.com/fortnite-item-shop/fortnite-march-14-2026-item-shop/); [Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/among-us-indie-cosmicube-bundle-goose-game-undertale-1851055845)
- Merchandising levers (Naavik, Jul 2022): rotating item shops create urgency (Rocket League). Battle passes create temporal exclusivity. PUBG Mobile is testing full-set completion rewards ("success remains to be seen"). Skin "evolution" upgrades (PUBG Mobile, Free Fire). Event bonuses for using a specific skin (COD Mobile, +50% event points). Rentals. Escalating-odds "Lucky Draw" gacha, which draws criticism. — [Naavik Part 2](https://naavik.co/f2p-mobile/selling-cosmetics-part-2/)
- Collection progression on skins: the Liquid & Grit headline says adding progression to skin collections boosted revenue 25% (Mobile Legends). Data is paywalled. — [Liquid & Grit](https://www.liquidandgrit.com/market-coverage/page/3/)
- Collection albums in casual and casino-adjacent games: Solitaire Grand Harvest runs time-limited albums and collab albums with exclusive card sets (2025). — [Senal News](https://senalnews.com/en/content/the-smurfs-make-their-way-to-solitaire-grand-harvest-cards-game)
- Motivation (Naavik, Jun 2022): self-expression and identity, differentiation (not looking default), status and prestige (e.g. Fortnite Season 1 skins, COD camos for completing the pass), aesthetics and variety, social connection and peer influence, trading. — [Naavik Part 1](https://naavik.co/deep-dives/selling-cosmetics-part1/)
- Survey data cited by Naavik: players are 3x more likely to consider spending on cosmetics than on power, and 6% would never buy cosmetics (Qutee). Newzoo (2020): 85% of US gamers aged 13–45 know about skins, 58% have engaged with them, 39% have paid real money, and big spenders average more than $15 a month. — [Naavik Part 1](https://naavik.co/deep-dives/selling-cosmetics-part1/)
- Google: vanity items cost more to make than consumables but put less strain on the economy. — [Google Play, Sep 2021](https://medium.com/googleplaydev/how-battle-passes-can-boost-engagement-and-monetization-in-your-game-d296dee6ddf8)

### Inferences
- A 4–5 step rarity ladder (Common / Rare / Epic / Legendary, plus optional Mythic or prestige) with roughly 2x price steps matches the evidence. Fortnite runs about 1.5–2.5x from bottom to top for outfits; Brawl Stars runs about 10x from Rare to Legendary.
- Themed sets (felt, card back, chips, coach outfit) with a set-completion bonus combine collection, self-expression and seasonal motives in one product. Album mechanics are already common in casino-adjacent casual games.
- For an 18+ gambling-adjacent education app, the rotation, rarity and FOMO techniques above should be used with care. Earned-only currency and no randomized paid boxes avoid the gacha criticism Naavik records. Permanent or returning catalogs, as with Hearthstone backs returning for gold, reduce FOMO pressure.

### Gaps
- No public evidence that set-completion bonuses raise purchases. Naavik calls PUBG's test unproven, and the Liquid & Grit 25% figure is paywalled.
- No 2024–2026 survey on motives for buying cosmetics specifically in card or casino games.

## 5. Analyst data on cosmetic monetization share (Sensor Tower, Deconstructor of Fun, GameRefinery/Liquid & Grit, Naavik)

### Takeaway
Analysts measure how many games use cosmetics, not how much revenue cosmetics earn. Decorative skins are in about 44% of the top-grossing 20% of US mobile games and 70% of top games have cosmetic or unlock purchases. Battle passes are 1–40% of revenue in top games. No credible public figure for "cosmetics' share of mobile game revenue" was found.

### Cited Findings
- GameRefinery (2021): decorative skins appear in 44% of the top-grossing 20% of US games. For China, Naavik reports 77% while GamesBeat reports 50% for the same data, so the sources conflict. — [Naavik Part 1, Jun 2022](https://naavik.co/deep-dives/selling-cosmetics-part1/); contradicted by [GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/5-features-that-drive-revenue-for-mobile-games-in-both-the-u-s-and-china/)
- GameRefinery: cosmetics now earn meaningful revenue in match-3 and casual games, not just shooters. — [WN Hub](https://wnhub.io/news/finance/item-44845)
- GameRefinery: 51% of US top-100 grossing games have a battle pass (2021). Google: about 30 of the top 100 on Play (2021). Appfigures: about half of top games (2025). — [GameRefinery](https://www.gamerefinery.com/7-tips-to-create-and-improve-battle-pass/); [Google Play](https://medium.com/googleplaydev/how-battle-passes-can-boost-engagement-and-monetization-in-your-game-d296dee6ddf8); [Appfigures](https://appfigures.com/resources/this-week-in-apps/20250516)
- Sensor Tower: 2024 global mobile game IAP revenue was about $82B, and in-game sessions rose 12% year over year. Hybrid-casual games led growth with +37% revenue (secondary roundup). No cosmetic split is published. — [Sensor Tower State of Mobile Gaming 2025](https://sensortower.com/blog/state-of-mobile-gaming-2025); [Appfigures roundup](https://appfigures.com/resources/this-week-in-apps/20250516)
- Newzoo (2020, cited by Naavik): estimated US skin market of about $40B a year. This is a broad estimate covering all platforms and should be treated cautiously. — [Naavik Part 1](https://naavik.co/deep-dives/selling-cosmetics-part1/)

### Inferences
- Treat cosmetics as a nearly universal feature in top games. Their revenue share is unknown publicly and likely varies widely by genre.

### Gaps
- No 2022–2026 public figure from Sensor Tower, Naavik or Liquid & Grit for cosmetics' share of revenue, and none specific to card, casino or chess games. Liquid & Grit's monthly casino and casual reports are paywalled.
