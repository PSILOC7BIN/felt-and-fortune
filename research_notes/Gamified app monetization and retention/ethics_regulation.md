# Ethics and Regulation of Monetizing a Gamified (Blackjack) Education App

Research compiled 2026-11-24. US focus with notes on UK and EU. All regulatory statuses are dated. The sources are mostly law-firm client alerts and FTC pages. Some blog sources on loot boxes are low quality and are flagged where they appear.

## 1. FTC "Click-to-Cancel" Negative Option Rule, ROSCA, and State Auto-Renewal Laws

### Takeaway
The 2024 federal Click-to-Cancel amendments were vacated by the 8th Circuit in July 2025. The FTC did not appeal. It opened a new rulemaking with an Advance Notice of Proposed Rulemaking (ANPRM, announced 2026-03-11), but no proposed or final rule existed as of the sources found. ROSCA (federal) and state auto-renewal laws (California since July 2025, New York since Nov 2025, NYC from 2026-10-01) are binding now. Together they require clear disclosure before billing, express affirmative consent, cancellation as easy as sign-up, and reminders before trials convert and before annual renewals.

### Cited Findings
- **Vacatur.** The 8th Circuit vacated the 2024 amendments in July 2025 on procedural grounds: the FTC skipped a preliminary regulatory analysis the statute required. The FTC reinstated the 1973 text, which covers only prenotification plans, and did not appeal — [Gibson Dunn](https://www.gibsondunn.com/ftc-restarts-negative-option-rulemaking-after-eighth-circuit-vacatur-enforcement-under-rosca-continues/); [Consumer Financial Services Law Monitor](https://www.consumerfinancialserviceslawmonitor.com/2026/03/ftc-reopens-negative-option-rulemaking-after-eight-circuit-vacates-2024-amendments/)
- **New rulemaking.**
  - The FTC sent a draft ANPRM to OIRA on 2026-01-30.
  - It announced the ANPRM on 2026-03-11, and comments were due 2026-04-13 — [Gibson Dunn](https://www.gibsondunn.com/ftc-restarts-negative-option-rulemaking-after-eighth-circuit-vacatur-enforcement-under-rosca-continues/)
  - The notice appeared in the Federal Register in 2026 — [Federal Register](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/full_text/xml/2026/02/12/2026-02866.xml)
  - The ANPRM has no draft rule text. It asks whether a new rule is needed at all, whether to update the 1973 rule, or whether to write a broader one — [Covington](https://www.cov.com/en/news-and-insights/insights/2026/03/ftc-launches-new-rulemaking-on-the-negative-option-rule); [Crowell](https://www.crowell.com/en/insights/client-alerts/clicking-all-the-right-boxes-ftc-moves-to-revive-click-to-cancel-rule-following-eighth-circuit-vacatur)
- **Kelley Drye's caution on the ANPRM.**
  - No new requirements take effect from it, and it does not guarantee a final rule.
  - The previous ANPRM (2019) took about five years to become a final rule — [Kelley Drye](https://www.kelleydrye.com/viewpoints/blogs/ad-law-access/ftc-relaunches-negative-option-rulemaking-with-new-questions-new-branding)
- **ROSCA requirements (in force).** For online negative-option features, ROSCA requires:
  - clear and conspicuous disclosure of all material terms *before* obtaining billing information;
  - the consumer's express informed consent;
  - a simple mechanism to stop recurring charges — [Gibson Dunn](https://www.gibsondunn.com/ftc-restarts-negative-option-rulemaking-after-eighth-circuit-vacatur-enforcement-under-rosca-continues/)
- **ROSCA enforcement continues.** It includes ed-tech and digital services. Gibson Dunn describes a $7.5M resolution over concealed cancellation options and a $60M settlement over renewal disclosures and refunds, without naming the companies. In November 2025, 34 states reached a $4.8M settlement over a paid membership program — [Gibson Dunn](https://www.gibsondunn.com/ftc-restarts-negative-option-rulemaking-after-eighth-circuit-vacatur-enforcement-under-rosca-continues/)
- **California ARL (AB 2863, effective 2025-07-01).** It applies to contracts entered, amended, or extended on or after that date. It requires:
  - express affirmative consent to the auto-renewal terms;
  - coverage of free-to-paid conversions;
  - an annual reminder for annual plans;
  - "click to cancel" online cancellation;
  - clear and conspicuous notice of material price or service changes.
  - A private right of action is possible via the UCL — [Cooley](https://www.cooley.com/news/insight/2025/2025-06-04-california-automatic-renewal-law-amendments-take-effect-on-july-1-2025); [Barnes & Thornburg](https://btlaw.com/en/insights/alerts/2025/california-expands-automatic-renewal-law-new-requirements-now-in-effect)
  - The amendments also include a "one save" limit on retention offers during cancellation — [Venable](https://www.venable.com/insights/publications/2024/09/california-amends-autorenewal-law-with-stricter)
- **New York (Part W of the budget bill, signed 2025-05-09, effective November 2025).**
  - Material terms must be disclosed before consent or billing.
  - For free trials or gifts longer than one month, the consumer gets a notice 3–21 days before the cancellation deadline, through their chosen contact method.
  - The cancellation mechanism must be as simple as sign-up and use the same medium.
  - Price increases need 5–30 days' notice. The business must either get affirmative consent to the increase or allow cancellation within 14 days with a pro-rata refund.
  - Save offers are allowed only if they don't obstruct or delay cancellation — [Kelley Drye](https://www.kelleydrye.com/viewpoints/blogs/ad-law-access/ny-quietly-amends-automatic-renewal-law)
- **New York City.** A city Click-to-Cancel rule takes effect 2026-10-01 — [Skadden](https://www.skadden.com/insights/publications/2026/09/new-york-citys-click-to-cancel-rule)
- **Other states.** Colorado and Minnesota have also strengthened disclosure, reminder, and cancellation rules. California's Automatic Renewal Task Force reached a $7.5M settlement with a meal-delivery service — [Gibson Dunn](https://www.gibsondunn.com/ftc-restarts-negative-option-rulemaking-after-eighth-circuit-vacatur-enforcement-under-rosca-continues/)

### Inferences
- Build to the strictest common denominator, which in practice means the CA and NY rules plus ROSCA:
  - itemized terms (price, frequency, renewal date, how to cancel) next to the purchase button, before payment details;
  - an unchecked consent box or a separate affirmative action;
  - a confirmation email with the terms;
  - a reminder 3–21 days before a trial converts (this window fits NY and is consistent with the others);
  - an annual-renewal reminder;
  - in-app, web, and store cancellation in no more steps than sign-up, with at most one save offer.
- In-app subscriptions bought through Apple or Google are billed and cancelled through the stores. The developer is still responsible for the disclosures and consent screen inside its own app.

### Gaps
- I could not confirm whether the FTC has published a proposed rule (NPRM) between April and November 2026. The writer should say "ANPRM stage as of the latest sources found (March–April 2026)."
- I did not retrieve the 8th Circuit case caption. It is generally reported as *Custom Communications, Inc. v. FTC*, but this is unverified here.
- I did not confirm the exact day in November 2025 that New York's changes took effect, or the details of the NYC rule.

## 2. FTC Dark-Patterns Guidance and Virtual-Currency Enforcement (Epic, HoYoverse)

### Takeaway
The FTC treats three things as deceptive or unfair: confusing virtual-currency systems, misleading odds, unconsented one-tap charges, and dark patterns that hide that a subscription renews. HoYoverse ($20M, Jan 2025) and Epic ($245M refunds plus $275M COPPA penalty, finalized 2023) are the models. For gems, energy, and limited-time offers, show the real-money price, avoid multi-step currency exchange, keep urgency claims true, and require deliberate confirmation before a charge.

### Cited Findings
- **"Bringing Dark Patterns to Light" (FTC staff report, Sept 2022).** It came out of an April 2021 workshop and was approved 5-0. It found dark patterns in e-commerce, cookie banners, **children's apps**, and subscription sales — [FTC report PDF](https://www.ftc.gov/system/files/ftc_gov/pdf/P214800%20Dark%20Patterns%20Report%209.14.2022%20-%20FINAL.pdf); [Venable](https://www.venable.com/insights/blogs/2022/09/the-ftc-brings-more-light-to-dark-patterns-in-new)
  - Its four categories:
    - misleading consumers;
    - hiding or delaying material information (including drip pricing);
    - design that leads to unintended payments;
    - subverting privacy choices — [Arnold & Porter](https://www.arnoldporter.com/en/perspectives/advisories/2022/09/ftc-shines-a-little-light-on-dark-patterns); [ZwillGen](https://www.zwillgen.com/ftc-state-ag/ftc-report-signals-doubling-down-on-enforcement-against-dark-patterns/)
  - It flags hiding that a free trial converts to paid, failing to disclose recurring charges, and hard-to-cancel flows — [ZwillGen](https://www.zwillgen.com/ftc-state-ag/ftc-report-signals-doubling-down-on-enforcement-against-dark-patterns/)
- **HoYoverse / Genshin Impact (announced Jan 2025, $20M, proposed order pending court approval at the time).**
  - **Allegations:**
    - loot-box odds were presented so players thought top prizes were likelier than they were;
    - real money passed through multiple currencies and exchange rates, so kids and teens didn't understand what they spent;
    - selling bundled, multi-tiered currency and loot boxes to minors was unfair;
    - COPPA violations.
  - **Order:**
    - no loot-box sales to under-16s without parental consent;
    - odds and exchange-rate disclosures;
    - loot boxes sold for virtual currency must also be buyable directly with US dollars;
    - deletion of under-13 data.
  - **FTC's business lessons:** avoid confusing currency systems, know how far COPPA reaches, and be truthful about odds and prices — [FTC business blog](https://ftc.gov/business-guidance/blog/2025/01/level-tips-businesses-ftcs-settlement-genshin-impact-developer-hoyoverse); [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/rpg/ftc-says-genshin-impact-deceived-children-and-orders-its-publisher-to-pay-a-usd20-million-fine-and-stop-selling-loot-boxes-to-kids)
- **Epic Games / Fortnite (announced Dec 2022, finalized Mar 2023).**
  - Epic paid $245M in refunds for dark patterns that tricked players into unwanted purchases. The FTC also alleged Epic locked the accounts of users who disputed charges. The order bars charging through dark patterns or without affirmative consent — [9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2023/03/15/ftc-finalizes-epic-games-245m-penalty-over-tricking-users-heres-how-to-get-your-refund/); [GameSpot](https://www.gamespot.com/amp-articles/epic-games-agrees-to-pay-500-million-in-fortnite-ftc-case/1100-6510151/)
  - Epic separately paid a $275M COPPA penalty, for $520M total — [PBS NewsHour](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/nation/fortnite-maker-epic-games-to-settle-ftc-case-pay-520-million-in-penalties-and-refunds)
  - The FTC sent over $72M in refunds in December 2024, averaging about $114 — [FTC press release](https://ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2024/12/ftc-sends-refund-payments-consumers-impacted-epic-games-unlawful-billing-practices)

### Inferences
- **Gems and coins:** use one currency, and show the dollar price next to every gem price. Avoid pack sizes that always leave leftovers. Allow direct dollar purchase of anything sold for gems, mirroring the HoYoverse order.
- **Energy and hearts refills:** state the dollar cost. Require a confirm step rather than one tap. Never refuse to let users dispute charges or lock their accounts for doing so, which was Epic's conduct.
- **Limited-time offers:** fake countdown timers or false scarcity fall under the report's "misleading" category. Use real end dates only, and don't reset timers.

### Gaps
- The full text of the Epic complaint (button layout, purchases by kids) was not fetched, so the specific mechanics are not cited here.
- I did not confirm whether the court approved the HoYoverse order.

## 3. Loot Boxes and Randomized Rewards (Belgium, Netherlands, UK, EU, US)

### Takeaway
Paid random rewards are treated as gambling in Belgium. Elsewhere they are lightly or self-regulated: the UK relies on voluntary industry principles with poor compliance, the EU is preparing a Digital Fairness Act, and the US has no federal statute but does have FTC and state AG enforcement. PEGI 16 default ratings for paid random items are reported from June 2026. For a blackjack-adjacent app, the safest course is no paid randomness at all. If reward chests exist, make them free and earned, and disclose their odds.

### Cited Findings
- **Belgium.** Paid loot boxes have been treated as illegal gambling since 2018, with criminal fines reported up to €800,000. A January 2025 case reportedly extended liability to app stores — [Blask (secondary, unverified)](https://blask.com/knowledge/loot-box/); [Promise Legal](https://blog.promise.legal/lootbox-regulation-2026-game-studios/)
- **Netherlands.** Sources conflict. One says the Netherlands ruled the mechanic is not gambling. Another says the government prefers EU-level regulation and that no ban is expected before 2026 — [Blask](https://blask.com/knowledge/loot-box/); [Programming Insider](https://programminginsider.com/loot-boxes-regulation-and-where-the-line-sits-in-2026/) (both secondary)
- **UK.**
  - Ukie published 11 industry principles in July 2023 after DCMS chose self-regulation. They commit to technological controls so that under-18s can't buy paid loot boxes without parental consent — [CMS](https://cms.law/en/gbr/legal-updates/loot-boxes-11-new-principles-for-an-industry-led-approach); [GOV.UK update](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/loot-boxes-in-video-games-update-on-improvements-to-industry-led-protections)
  - A May 2025 Royal Society report found widespread non-compliance — [Parallel Parliament, Lords debate 2026-01-15](https://parallelparliament.co.uk/debate/2026-01-15/lords/lords-chamber/in-game-purchases-protections-for-children)
  - 5Rights called enforcement "non-existent" — [5Rights](https://5rightsfoundation.com/research-reveals-non-existent-enforcement-of-industry-led-standards-on-loot-boxes/)
  - In the January 2026 Lords debate, further academic evaluation was described as forthcoming — [Parallel Parliament](https://parallelparliament.co.uk/debate/2026-01-15/lords/lords-chamber/in-game-purchases-protections-for-children)
- **EU and PEGI.**
  - PEGI reportedly assigns a default PEGI 16 rating to new submissions with paid random items from June 2026 — [Blask](https://blask.com/knowledge/loot-box/); [Shattered (low-quality blog)](https://shattered.io/loot-box-pegi-16-crackdown-2026/)
  - A leaked Digital Fairness Act draft reportedly requires per-item probability disclosure and tools to disable loot boxes. Timing reports conflict (Q3 2026 vs. an 11 Nov 2026 presentation) — [Shattered](https://shattered.io/digital-fairness-act-leak-loot-boxes-minors-6-percent-fines-2026/); [Promise Legal](https://blog.promise.legal/lootbox-regulation-2026-game-studios/)
- **US.**
  - There is no federal loot-box statute. The New York AG reportedly sued Valve in February 2026 over paid boxes — [Blask (secondary)](https://blask.com/knowledge/loot-box/)
  - The FTC's HoYoverse order requires odds disclosure and parental consent for under-16s — [FTC](https://ftc.gov/business-guidance/blog/2025/01/level-tips-businesses-ftcs-settlement-genshin-impact-developer-hoyoverse)

### Inferences
- An app that teaches people to *resist* gambling would undercut its own message by selling paid random chests. The recommendation is:
  - deterministic or earned rewards only, with no randomized item bought with real money or with currency bought with real money;
  - published odds if chests are randomized at all;
  - no "near-miss" animations.

### Gaps
- Loot-box regulatory facts for Belgium, the Netherlands, PEGI, the EU DFA, and NY v. Valve come mostly from secondary or SEO blogs. Primary sources not verified:
  - Belgian Gaming Commission 2018 report;
  - Dutch Raad van State 2022 EA ruling;
  - PEGI announcement;
  - European Commission DFA proposal;
  - NY AG complaint.
- I did not check US state loot-box bills for 2025–26.

## 4. Gambling-Adjacent Concerns: Simulated Gambling Research, Age Gating, COPPA

### Takeaway
Longitudinal evidence links youth simulated gambling to later real-money gambling and to problem gambling, with the strongest signal for **card games and poker**, which are the closest analog to blackjack. Causation is contested. Apple's 2025 rating system puts frequent simulated gambling at 18+. The amended COPPA Rule has been fully in force since 2026-04-22. A blackjack trainer should therefore be 18+ (21+ where marketing references US casinos), carry responsible-gambling messaging, and avoid under-13 users.

### Cited Findings
- **Poker.** Adolescents who played simulated poker were almost 5x more likely to have moved to real-money gambling at follow-up. A later analysis found that baseline simulated gambling predicted later monetary gambling *only* for card-based poker — [Kim/King/Delfabbro et al. longitudinal study, ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/318152965_Transition_from_playing_with_simulated_gambling_games_to_gambling_with_real_money_a_longitudinal_study_in_adolescence); [ResearchGate (Do simulated gambling activities predict…)](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/323128073_Do_Simulated_Gambling_Activities_Predict_Gambling_with_Real_Money_During_Adolescence_Empirical_Findings_from_a_Longitudinal_Study). Author attribution was not confirmed in snippets; see Gaps.
- **Germany.** In a longitudinal study of 12–17-year-olds, simulated gambling in video games directly predicted problem gambling one year later. Adolescents who play *and spend money* in simulated gambling games are at heightened risk — [PMC9517771](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC9517771/)
- **Order of first play.** Free loot boxes and games with gambling content were significantly more likely to *precede* monetary gambling forms — [Journal of Behavioral Addictions 2023](https://doi.org/10.1556/2006.2023.00069)
- **Replications and reviews.**
  - A 2025 British Columbia replication tested migration from loot boxes to gambling — [PMC12044733](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC12044733/)
  - A 2025 New Zealand Ministry of Health review covers the convergence of simulated and monetary gambling — [Hing et al.](https://www.health.govt.nz/system/files/2025-12/hing-et-al-convergence-simulated-gambling-monetary-gambling.pdf)
  - The Australian government review found co-occurrence but not a proven transition effect for loot boxes. It found some evidence of migration from simulated gambling to monetary gambling — [Australian Classification review 2022](https://www.classification.gov.au/sites/default/files/documents/agrc_literature_review_final_20220906_accessible.pdf)
- **Youth perception.** Qualitative research reports adolescents saying simulated gambling "doesn't give off the gambling vibes" — [ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/372486672_'It_Doesn't_Give_Off_the_Gambling_Vibes_It_Just_Feels_Like_a_Part_of_the_Game'_Adolescents'_Experiences_and_Perceptions_of_Simulated_Gambling_While_Growing_Up)
- **App stores.**
  - Apple's 2025 age-rating overhaul added 13+, 16+, and 18+ tiers. Frequent simulated gambling falls in the 18+ tier, and infrequent simulated gambling gets a lower-tier descriptor. Australia adds an R18+ regional rating for simulated gambling — [Apple App Store Connect Help](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/age-ratings); [Neowin](https://www.neowin.net/news/simulated-gambling-triggers-r18-rating-in-australia-for-iphone-apps/)
  - Google Play handles simulated gambling ratings through the IARC questionnaire — [Google Play policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9877032); [Google Play ratings](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/6209544?hl=en)
- **COPPA.**
  - The amended Rule was published 2025-04-22, effective 2025-06-23, with full compliance required by 2026-04-22. It requires:
    - separate verifiable parental consent before disclosing children's data to third parties for targeted ads;
    - a written data-retention policy;
    - a written information-security program;
    - expanded notices — [Federal Register](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2025/04/22/2025-05904/childrens-online-privacy-protection-rule); [Davis Polk](https://www.davispolk.com/insights/client-update/ftc-prioritizes-coppa-enforcement-new-compliance-obligations-take-effect); [Latham](https://www.lw.com/en/insights/ftc-publishes-updates-to-coppa-rule)
  - COPPA applies even to services not aimed at children if they use child-appealing content (such as animated characters) or have actual knowledge of under-13 users — [FTC](https://ftc.gov/business-guidance/blog/2025/01/level-tips-businesses-ftcs-settlement-genshin-impact-developer-hoyoverse)

### Inferences
- **Age:** rate the app 18+ with a neutral age gate (date-of-birth entry, not "Are you 18? Yes"). Avoid cartoon mascots that pull in children. Use 21+ for any casino-affiliate or real-money references in the US.
- **Responsible-gambling content:**
  - state that the house edge persists even with perfect basic strategy;
  - make clear that card counting is not a money-making plan for most people;
  - link to the 1-800-GAMBLER / NCPG helpline;
  - provide a self-exclusion or "take a break" setting.
- **Virtual chips:** never convertible to cash, and never purchasable in ways that mimic buying a stake.

### Gaps
- I did not retrieve NCPG's or the UK Gambling Commission's specific guidance for social or simulated gambling apps, or NCPG's helpline wording. Use the standard 1-800-GAMBLER, but verify it.
- The author attribution of the poker longitudinal study (Kim, Wohl, Gupta, Derevensky are likely) was not verified. The King & Delfabbro and Zendle works were not individually retrieved.

## 5. Duolingo Critiques and Ethical Gamification Frameworks

### Takeaway
Duolingo's 2025 move from Hearts to Energy, which charges energy per exercise even for correct answers in its reported form, drew visible backlash from long-time users. Streaks are effective but rely on loss aversion. The ethical gamification literature identifies psychological distress and exploitation as the main harms. It also shows that disclosure alone doesn't neutralize manipulative patterns.

### Cited Findings
- **Energy system.**
  - Free users get about 25 energy units, spent per exercise and recharging over time or via ads. Super and Max subscribers get unlimited energy. The rollout began in spring 2025 as an A/B test — [DuoPlanet](https://duoplanet.com/duolingo-energy-system/); [AOL/News](https://www.aol.com/news/duolingo-bringing-energy-language-learning-120004759.html)
  - Users described feeling "punished." Reports cite a 700-day streak holder quitting and a poll of more than 11,000 people in which about half disliked Energy (blog-reported figures) — [TechIssuesToday](https://techissuestoday.com/duolingo-energy-system-user-backlash/); [DuoPlanet](https://duoplanet.com/duolingo-energy-system/)
  - Duolingo says beginners were twice as likely to run out under Hearts, and that learners "do more" under Energy — [DuoPlanet](https://duoplanet.com/duolingo-energy-system/)
- **Streak redesign.** A Duolingo streak change (one lesson keeps the streak) reportedly raised the number of users with 7+ day streaks by about 40% while adding recovery options. This is a vendor-side account and not peer-reviewed — [Designfolio](https://designfolio.substack.com/p/crazy-ux-redesign-duolingo); [UX Collective](https://uxdesign.cc/beyond-streaks-and-nudges-ethical-gamification-ai-design-1c4f6e8ef2cc)
- **Ethics literature.**
  - A systematic review (Tampere University) finds psychological distress and exploitation dominate the gamification-harms literature — [Tampere](https://trepo.tuni.fi/bitstream/10024/146603/1/0107.pdf)
  - A 2026 Untappd longitudinal study notes that ethical gamification frameworks exist but are applied unevenly — [arXiv 2601.04841](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.04841v1)
  - Dark patterns are defined as designs that make people do things they otherwise wouldn't, exploiting cognitive biases — [arXiv 2503.01828](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2503.01828)
  - Recognizing game dark patterns does not reliably reduce harmful engagement — [Bournemouth / PACM HCI](https://eprints.bournemouth.ac.uk/42451/1/PACMHCIv10i7-4290.pdf)

### Inferences
- **Hearts or energy:** if used, charge only for *mistakes* (as Hearts did), never for correct practice. Provide a free, practice-based way to refill, for example a review lesson. Never block mid-lesson.
- **Streaks:** offer free streak freezes and "weekend off" options. Keep notifications neutral and avoid guilt-trips. Make notifications easy to mute, with a frequency cap.
- **Ethical-gamification checklist:**
  - the user's goal (learning) is the success metric, not session time;
  - no loss-framed nagging;
  - transparency about the costs of mechanics;
  - autonomy (opt-out of competitive or social features).

### Gaps
- No primary Duolingo data on Energy retention or revenue impact.
- The "43% streak anxiety" stat circulating online could not be verified and should not be used.
- Named frameworks (Tondello & Nacke 2018; Mora et al. 2015 "Dark Side of Gamification") were not retrieved.

## 6. Paywall Accessibility and Fairness (Pay-to-Win, Locked Essentials)

### Takeaway
There is no specific US law against pay-to-win leaderboards. The fairness principles come from FTC unfairness doctrine and ethics guidance. Keep core educational and responsible-gambling content free, and keep leaderboards skill-only.

### Cited Findings
- Selling bundled, multi-tiered currency to minors who can't judge the value they are paying for was alleged as *unfair* — [FTC HoYoverse blog](https://ftc.gov/business-guidance/blog/2025/01/level-tips-businesses-ftcs-settlement-genshin-impact-developer-hoyoverse)
- Dark patterns include designs that lead to unintended payments — [Arnold & Porter on FTC report](https://www.arnoldporter.com/en/perspectives/advisories/2022/09/ftc-shines-a-little-light-on-dark-patterns)

### Inferences
- **Free tier:** keep basic strategy, house-edge explanations, and responsible-gambling and helpline content free and unlimited, never behind energy limits.
- **Leaderboards:** rank on accuracy or skill metrics that can't be bought (no XP boosts that count toward rank). Or separate the "boosted" and "pure" boards.
- **Premium:** sell convenience, depth (advanced counting drills, analytics), and cosmetics, not competitive advantage or safety information.
- **Accessibility:** paywall screens need screen-reader labels, visible close buttons (no hidden X), and a price in plain text. A hidden close button would fall under the FTC's "unintended payments" category.

### Gaps
- No law or regulator guidance specific to pay-to-win in educational apps was found.
- I did not research WCAG requirements for paywalls specifically, or the ADA case law on app accessibility.
