# Duolingo Business Model, Pricing, and In-App Economy (2023 to late 2026)

Research date: 2026-11-24. Primary sources are Duolingo shareholder letters filed with the SEC as 8-K exhibits. Duolingo's IR site (investors.duolingo.com) returned HTTP 403 to automated fetches, so the Q3 2025 and Q4 2025 letters could not be read directly and their figures come from press coverage. Ratios marked "derived" are my arithmetic on reported figures, not company-stated numbers.

## Subscription tiers (Super, Max, Family): prices, unlocks, trials, changes over time

### Takeaway
Duolingo has run on a freemium model with one core paid tier (Super, about $12.99/mo or $83.99/yr in the US) plus a premium AI tier (Max, about $29.99/mo or $167.99/yr) and family plans for up to 6 accounts. In Q3 2026 Duolingo began **sunsetting Max**: it stopped new Max sales, is moving Video Call into Super, and is converting existing individual Max subscribers to a Super Family plan at the same price. Duolingo publishes no official US price list, so all dollar prices come from third parties.

### Cited Findings
- **Max launched in March 2023**, powered by GPT-4, with two AI features: Explain My Answer and Roleplay. — [TechCrunch, 2023-03-14](https://techcrunch.com/2023/03/14/duolingo-launches-new-subscription-tier-with-access-to-ai-tutor-powered-by-gpt-4); [Duolingo blog](https://blog.duolingo.com/duolingo-max)
- **Max launch price: $29.99/month or $167.99/year** (US). — [ghacks, 2023-03-21](https://www.ghacks.net/2023/03/21/duolingo-max-vs-super-gpt-4-powered-difference/); [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/duolingos-max-plan-offers-ai-tutoring-for-30-per-month-203625446.html?src=rss)
- **Super, US, Sept–Oct 2026 (third-party observations):** about $12.99/month and **$83.99/year** on US Google Play (seen 2026-09-27), which works out to about $7/month after a 14-day free trial. One US account in Oct 2026 saw Super at $16.99/month and $119.99/year, with Super Family at $143.99/year. — [search summary of dealnews / familypro / mindomax trackers](https://familypro.io/en/blog/duolingo-max-vs-super); [dealnews](https://www.dealnews.com/features/duolingo/cost). (Conflicting observations. Prices vary by platform, A/B test, and promotion.)
- **Super Family: about $119.99/year for up to 6 accounts** (the most common US figure). One observation was $143.99/year. — [FamilyPro](https://familypro.io/en/blog/duolingo-max-vs-super); [dealnews](https://www.dealnews.com/features/duolingo/cost)
- **Max Family: about $239.99/year, annual billing only.** — [familypro](https://familypro.io/en/blog/duolingo-max-vs-super); [duolingoguides](https://duolingoguides.com/?p=7322)
- **What Super unlocks:** no ads, unlimited Hearts/Energy (subscribers cannot turn unlimited Energy off), free Legendary challenges (otherwise about 100 gems), and, as of 2026, Video Call. — [The Verge via mirror, 2025-05-12](https://thevergetoday.pages.dev/news/665315/duolingo-hearts-energy-system); [AOL/energy coverage](https://www.aol.com/news/duolingo-bringing-energy-language-learning-120004759.html); [Q2 2026 letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001562088/000162828026053299/q2fy26duolingo6-30x26share.htm)
- **Free trial:** 14 days is the standard for new users. Some accounts are offered 7-day or 3-day trials depending on account history, location, or promotion. — [trybeem 2026 guide](https://trybeem.com/blog/how-to-get-duolingo-free-trial/); [dealnews](https://dealnews.com/Super-Duolingo-Subscription-Free-2-week-trial/21717990.html)
- **2026 shift toward longer trials:** the Q2 2026 letter says the company is "offering longer free trials, which we expect to increase both revenue and DAUs." The CEO repeated "longer free trials" as a monetization tactic on the Q3 2026 call. — [Q2 2026 letter (SEC)](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001562088/000162828026053299/q2fy26duolingo6-30x26share.htm); [CX Dive, 2026-11-05](https://www.customerexperiencedive.com/news/duolingo-ceo-monetize-without-degrading-free-user-experience/873877/)
- **2025 price increase:** the Q2 2026 letter says the year-ago quarter (Q2 2025) included "a price increase," which made the bookings comparison harder. The CFO said on the Q3 2025 call that "we did take pricing at various times." — [Q2 2026 letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001562088/000162828026053299/q2fy26duolingo6-30x26share.htm); [Q3 2025 call transcript, Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2025/11/06/duolingo-duol-q3-2025-earnings-call-transcript)
- **Pricing tests both ways:** von Ahn said on the Q3 2025 call, "you'll see us test prices. And by that, I mean, up and down." He floated a possible "half price" super-light package as a test idea, not a decision. — [Fool transcript](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2025/11/06/duolingo-duol-q3-2025-earnings-call-transcript)
- **Explain My Answer became free to all learners around 2026-01-01** in major courses (Spanish, French, German, Japanese, Portuguese, Italian, Korean). — [search summary of third-party Max explainers, e.g. beginnersinai](https://beginnersinai.org/duolingo-max-explained/) (third-party claim, not confirmed by a primary source)
- **Video Call moves into Super:** the Q4 2025 results (2026-02-26) said Video Call would move into Super and that Duolingo would experiment with the Max tier. Management called this an offensive move, not one driven by weakness. The Q1 2026 letter says it is "starting to expand access to Video Call to Super subscribers." The Q2 2026 letter says "most new Super Duolingo subscribers now have access to Video Call," with existing subscribers getting it later in the year. — [Yahoo Q4 call highlights](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/duolingo-q4-earnings-call-highlights-041400802.html); [Q1 2026 letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001562088/000162828026029790/q1fy26duolingo3-31x26share.htm); [Q2 2026 letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001562088/000162828026053299/q2fy26duolingo6-30x26share.htm)
- **Max sunset (Q3 2026 letter, 2026-11-03):** Duolingo stopped new Max sales. Existing individual Max subscribers move to a Super family plan at the same price, and legacy Max Family subscribers will be handled in 2027. Management expects minimal impact on bookings. Unlimited Video Call is expected to reach all Super subscribers by year-end 2026. — [Q3 2026 letter (SEC)](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001562088/000162828026069474/q3fy26duolingo9-30x26share.htm)
- **Family plans and Max raised ARPU:** in Q4 2024, a higher family-plan and Max mix drove a 2% YoY rise in subscription revenue per average subscriber. In 2025, ARPU rose mid-single digits every quarter, "mainly" from Max rather than list-price changes (CFO). — [Q4 2024 letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1562088/000156208825000039/q4fy24duolingo12-31x24shar.htm); [Fool Q3 2025 transcript](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2025/11/06/duolingo-duol-q3-2025-earnings-call-transcript)

### Inferences
- The tier ladder has gone from three tiers (Super, Max, Family) back toward one Super tier plus a family upsell. A separate premium AI tier did not scale: it peaked at about 9% of subscribers. Folding the best AI feature into the base tier became the preferred way to lift conversion.
- Annual pricing at roughly 45–55% off 12x monthly (e.g. $83.99/yr vs $12.99/mo) is the anchor. The monthly price exists mainly to make the annual plan look cheap.

### Gaps
- No official Duolingo US price list exists. The 2025 price increase is confirmed by the company, but its size and the before/after prices could not be found.
- How Max subscriptions were split between monthly and annual billing was never disclosed.

## Hearts vs Energy

### Takeaway
Hearts gave free users 5 lives, and each mistake cost one. Energy, announced in May–July 2025 and fully rolled out by late 2025, gives 25 units, and every exercise costs one whether right or wrong. You can earn energy back through answer streaks and perfect lessons. Duolingo says Energy "increased bookings" and "increased DAUs." Some vocal users pushed back (petitions, critical press).

### Cited Findings
- **How Hearts worked:** 5 hearts, each mistake costs one, and they refill over about a day. You could restore hearts by watching an ad, paying gems for a full refill, or waiting. — [The Verge via mirror, 2025-05-12](https://thevergetoday.pages.dev/news/665315/duolingo-hearts-energy-system)
- **How Energy works:** the bar holds 25 units and each exercise costs 1. It regenerates over time, and you can also buy energy with gems. Super and Max subscribers get unlimited energy. The rollout started on iOS in May 2025, with Android later in 2025. — [The Verge via mirror](https://thevergetoday.pages.dev/news/665315/duolingo-hearts-energy-system)
- **Official description (blog, 2025-07-03):** you start full and each lesson uses "a little bit." Energy fully recharges in about a day. You earn more by getting several answers right in a row, and bonus energy comes from perfect lessons, adding a friend, and reward chests. Reviewing mistakes at the end of a lesson costs no energy. When low, you can watch a rewarded ad for some energy or buy a refill with gems. — [Duolingo blog](https://blog.duolingo.com/duolingo-energy/)
- **Sources conflict on whether a mistake costs extra.** The Verge reads as "mistake also costs one energy," which may simply mean that the exercise costs one regardless. Other guides say wrong answers cost nothing extra. Duolingo's own engineer said mistakes are "no longer penalized." — [Verge mirror](https://thevergetoday.pages.dev/news/665315/duolingo-hearts-energy-system); [search summary](https://duolingoguides.com/duolingo-energy-system/)
- **Duolingo's stated reason:** under Hearts, beginners were about twice as likely to run out mid-lesson, which could discourage them. Energy "helps more learners complete lessons" (no figures given). Engineer Moses Wayne said that under Hearts a user could fail their first lesson of the day: "In an energy world, that's really not going to happen." — [Duolingo blog](https://blog.duolingo.com/duolingo-energy/); [Verge mirror](https://thevergetoday.pages.dev/news/665315/duolingo-hearts-energy-system)
- **Results (Q3 2025 call, 2025-11-05):** von Ahn said, "It did exactly what we wanted it to do. It increased bookings. And also increased DAUs." The CFO called energy "a core pricing mechanic." Energy had reached every user who updated the app in the prior 6–9 months. — [Fool transcript](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2025/11/06/duolingo-duol-q3-2025-earnings-call-transcript)
- **The 25-vs-24 tradeoff (von Ahn):** cutting daily energy from 25 to 24 "would make us more money" but would "decrease daily active users." Duolingo would not ship such a change if it had "a real hit on daily active users." — [Fool transcript](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2025/11/06/duolingo-duol-q3-2025-earnings-call-transcript)
- **Year-over-year comparisons:** the Q2 2026 letter cites "the initial rollout of Energy" in Q2 2025 as a tough comparison for bookings. That implies Energy caused a one-time boost to bookings. — [Q2 2026 letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001562088/000162828026053299/q2fy26duolingo6-30x26share.htm)
- **Gem refill prices for Energy vary:** about 750 gems for a full refill on iPhone and about 350 on Android, versus roughly 350–500 for Hearts. One guide says 350 gems (450 mid-lesson). These are third-party figures that vary with tests. — [duolingoguides](https://duolingoguides.com/duolingo-energy-system/); [search summaries](https://lukmayer.github.io/blog/posts/duolingo2)
- **Backlash:** an Android Authority writer said Energy means "I can no longer meaningfully learn as a free user." PCWorld wrote a "fell out of love" piece. Multiple change.org petitions call for restoring hearts. — [Android Authority](https://www.androidauthority.com/quitting-duolingo-energy-system-3599842/); [PCWorld](https://www.pcworld.com/article/2924235/why-i-fell-out-of-love-with-duolingo.html); [change.org](https://www.change.org/p/restore-the-hearts-feature-in-duolingo-and-remove-energy)

### Inferences
- Energy moves the paywall from penalizing skill (mistakes) to limiting volume (exercises per day). Beginners hit the wall less often, so retention is protected. Heavy, engaged users hit it more often, and those are the users most likely to pay. This explains how bookings and DAU could both rise.
- The falling In-App Purchases revenue line (−11% in Q1, −23% in Q2, −38% in Q3 2026 YoY; see metrics) suggests fewer gem/refill purchases after Energy and the 2026 friction reduction. This is not confirmed by the company.

### Gaps
- Duolingo published no quantified lift in conversion or DAU from Energy, and no exact regeneration rate per hour.
- Whether 2026 friction-reduction changes altered the 25-unit cap could not be confirmed.

## Gems economy

### Takeaway
Gems are a soft currency. You earn them from lessons, quests, chests, and leagues, and you can also buy them. You spend them on streak protection, refills, boosts, challenges, and cosmetics. Real-money In-App Purchases (including gems) are a small and shrinking revenue line: $6.9M (2.2% of revenue) in Q3 2026, down 38% YoY.

### Cited Findings
- **Gem shop prices (third-party, may vary):** Streak Freeze 200 gems; Timer Boost about 450 gems; Legendary challenge 100 gems (free with Super); Double or Nothing wager 50 gems (7-day streak returns 100); Heart/Energy refill about 350 gems (450 mid-lesson); Animated Status 500 gems; Streak Shield 3,000 gems (iOS only). — [search summary of third-party price tables](https://duolingoguides.com/?p=7381); [lingoly streak freeze guide](https://lingoly.io/duolingo-streak-freeze/)
- Web streak freezes were historically priced at 10 lingots. Lingots were the web currency before gems were unified. — [techcult](https://techcult.com/how-to-freeze-streak-on-duolingo)
- **Earning gems:** perfect lessons, adding friends, and reward chests also give bonus energy. Gems come from lessons, quests, chests, and league rewards. — [Duolingo blog energy post](https://blog.duolingo.com/duolingo-energy/); [Duolingo 101](https://blog.duolingo.com/duolingo-101-how-to-learn-a-language-on-duolingo)
- **In-App Purchases revenue (SEC):** Q1 2025 $9.4M → Q1 2026 $8.4M (−11%). Q2 2025 $10.4M → Q2 2026 $8.0M (−23%). Q3 2025 $11.1M → Q3 2026 $6.9M (−38%). — [Q1 2026](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001562088/000162828026029790/q1fy26duolingo3-31x26share.htm); [Q2 2026](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001562088/000162828026053299/q2fy26duolingo6-30x26share.htm); [Q3 2026](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001562088/000162828026069474/q3fy26duolingo9-30x26share.htm)
- **Cosmetics are a new IAP lever in 2026:** the CEO cited "charging for avatar suits." Avatar Collection events such as "Junior's Toy Hunt" are planned on a regular cadence to drive in-app purchases. — [Q3 2026 letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001562088/000162828026069474/q3fy26duolingo9-30x26share.htm); [CX Dive](https://www.customerexperiencedive.com/news/duolingo-ceo-monetize-without-degrading-free-user-experience/873877/)

### Inferences
- The gem economy works mainly as a retention and conversion device: streak protection, and refills that make subscribing look attractive. It is not a profit center (IAP is about 2–4% of revenue). For a small app, gems are worth more as an engagement loop and a nudge toward subscribing than as a direct revenue source.

### Gaps
- Real-money gem pack prices (USD) were not retrievable. The fandom wiki returned 402. No reliable XP Boost price or official outfit prices were found.

## Ads

### Takeaway
Advertising is about 7% of revenue (about $21M/quarter in 2026) and is flat YoY. Ads are the free tier's monetization, and removing them is a core Super benefit.

### Cited Findings
- **Advertising revenue:** Q1 2026 $20.6M (+15% YoY from $17.9M). Q2 2026 $21.1M (+2% from $20.6M). Q3 2026 $20.8M (−1% from $21.0M). — SEC letters [Q1](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001562088/000162828026029790/q1fy26duolingo3-31x26share.htm), [Q2](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001562088/000162828026053299/q2fy26duolingo6-30x26share.htm), [Q3](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001562088/000162828026069474/q3fy26duolingo9-30x26share.htm)
- **Ad share of revenue (derived):** Q1 2025 7.8%, Q2 2025 8.2%, Q3 2025 7.7%, Q1 2026 7.1%, Q2 2026 7.1%, Q3 2026 6.7%. — derived from the SEC letters above
- Q2 2025 had "advertising outperformance" (Q2 2026 letter). In Q4 2024, advertising revenue per DAU and ad gross margin declined. — [Q2 2026](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001562088/000162828026053299/q2fy26duolingo6-30x26share.htm); [Q4 2024](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1562088/000156208825000039/q4fy24duolingo12-31x24shar.htm)
- **Rewarded ads** let free users earn back energy (and previously hearts). — [Duolingo blog](https://blog.duolingo.com/duolingo-energy/); [Verge mirror](https://thevergetoday.pages.dev/news/665315/duolingo-hearts-energy-system)

### Inferences
- Ad revenue per DAU is falling. DAU grew 29% in Q3 2026 while ad revenue fell 1%. This is consistent with the 2026 strategy of cutting free-tier friction, and it may mean fewer ad slots per user (not confirmed).

### Gaps
- No primary source lists every ad placement (e.g., after-lesson interstitials). Industry knowledge says interstitials show after lessons for free users, but I found no citable 2026 source.

## Duolingo Max (AI features and pricing)

### Takeaway
Max ($29.99/mo, $167.99/yr at launch in 2023) bundled GPT-4 features: Explain My Answer, Roleplay, and from Q3 2024, Video Call with the character Lily. It grew to only 9% of subscribers by Q3 2025. Its features are now being given to Super or free users, and as of Q3 2026 the tier is being sunset.

### Cited Findings
- Launch features (March 2023): Explain My Answer and Roleplay. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2023/03/14/duolingo-launches-new-subscription-tier-with-access-to-ai-tutor-powered-by-gpt-4)
- Video Call was introduced in Q3 2024 and called "a key driver" of growth. — [Q4 2024 letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1562088/000156208825000039/q4fy24duolingo12-31x24shar.htm)
- **Max share of paid subscribers over time:** 5% at YE2024 ([Q4 2024 letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1562088/000156208825000039/q4fy24duolingo12-31x24shar.htm)), 7% in Q1 2025, 8% in Q2 2025 ([Q2 2025 call, AOL](https://www.aol.com/finance/duolingo-duol-q2-2025-earnings-230249111.html)), and 9% in Q3 2025 ([Fool Q3 2025 transcript](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2025/11/06/duolingo-duol-q3-2025-earnings-call-transcript)).
- **Q3 2025 performance:** Max bookings doubled YoY but were "underperforming our lofty expectations." Max renewed "slightly better than Super." Words spoken per Max subscriber more than doubled in 2025. Monolingual Video Call was "too hard" for beginners, so bilingual guided calls were added. — [Fool transcript](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2025/11/06/duolingo-duol-q3-2025-earnings-call-transcript)
- Max was being sunset as of 2026-11-03, as covered in the subscription tiers section. — [Q3 2026 letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001562088/000162828026069474/q3fy26duolingo9-30x26share.htm)
- The rollout of AI features costs margin: Q4 2026 gross margin guidance is 72.0%, 90 bps lower sequentially, tied to a wider rollout of AI-based features. — [Q3 2026 letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001562088/000162828026069474/q3fy26duolingo9-30x26share.htm)

### Inferences
- A 2.3x price premium for AI features capped adoption at about 1 in 11 subscribers. Duolingo concluded the AI features were worth more for raising base-tier conversion and retention than as a separate upsell, even though inference costs reduce gross margin.

### Gaps
- No 2026 Max share figure was disclosed before the sunset.

## Key metrics 2023–2026

### Takeaway
DAU went from 26.9M (Q4 2023) to 64.9M (Q3 2026). DAU/MAU rose from about 30% to about 42%. Paid subscribers went from 6.6M to 13.1M, but have stayed at roughly 8.5–9% of MAU. Revenue went from $531M (FY2023) to $1,038M (FY2025), with $1,221M guided for FY2026. Subscriptions are about 87% of revenue. Growth slowed sharply in 2026 (bookings +8–14%) after the deliberate pivot to user growth.

### Inferences
- Paid conversion has been very stable at about 8–9% of MAU across 2023–2026, while DAU/MAU rose about 12 points. Engagement mechanics (streaks, Energy) raised habit formation much more than they raised conversion rate. Revenue growth came from audience growth multiplied by a stable conversion rate, plus ARPU from Max and Family plans and price increases.
- For a small app, a reasonable mature benchmark from Duolingo is about 8–9% paid of MAU and about 40% DAU/MAU. Duolingo reached these with massive scale and constant experimentation, so they are a ceiling, not a baseline.

## Leadership statements on monetization philosophy

### Takeaway
Duolingo's constant line is that learning stays free and money comes from optional convenience and premium features, decided by heavy A/B testing. From Q3 2025 it explicitly put DAU growth and teaching quality ahead of near-term monetization. By late 2026 it was "gradually leaning more into monetization" under "a strict guardrail" against degrading the free tier.

### Cited Findings
- Von Ahn (Q3 2025 call): "we are going to be prioritizing user growth over monetization in this type of judgment call." He also said "the goal here is to be growing DAUs fast for a very long time." — [Fool transcript](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2025/11/06/duolingo-duol-q3-2025-earnings-call-transcript)
- CFO Matt Skaruppa (Q3 2025): "Whenever we fixate on a metric, we are very good at moving it." He also said "we are not going to prioritize linear margin expansion from here." An analyst noted September 2025 free-to-paid conversion was "slightly lower." — [Fool transcript](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2025/11/06/duolingo-duol-q3-2025-earnings-call-transcript)
- **2026 strategy (Q1 2026 letter):** "prioritizing teaching better and growing our audience" and "moving quickly to prioritize the product and free user experience." Better teaching "can drive engagement, retention, growth, and brand credibility." — [Q1'26 letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001562088/000162828026029790/q1fy26duolingo3-31x26share.htm)
- Q2 2026 letter: "finding ways to increase revenue without adding friction to free users." — [Q2'26 letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001562088/000162828026053299/q2fy26duolingo6-30x26share.htm)
- Q3 2026: "gradually leaning more into monetization in ways that preserve the quality of our free experience." Subscriptions are "the largest monetization opportunity," and better teaching is the most durable driver. Von Ahn cited "a strict guardrail" against degrading the free tier. — [Q3'26 letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001562088/000162828026069474/q3fy26duolingo9-30x26share.htm); [CX Dive](https://www.customerexperiencedive.com/news/duolingo-ceo-monetize-without-degrading-free-user-experience/873877/)
- **A/B testing culture:** hundreds of A/B tests run weekly through the "Green Machine" system, with experiment volume up about 90% YoY in late 2026. AI has shortened the time from idea to experiment. Retention drove most of the DAU gains. — [CX Dive](https://www.customerexperiencedive.com/news/duolingo-ceo-monetize-without-degrading-free-user-experience/873877/)
- FY2024 bookings growth came from "packaging, promotion, and conversion experiments." The company planned to increase "experiment velocity to make the experience more social and gamified." — [Q4 2024 letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1562088/000156208825000039/q4fy24duolingo12-31x24shar.htm)
- Von Ahn on gamification: "like a streak, it works in every country." The app "is gonna be a lot more social" (chess PvP). Chess retention is slightly higher than language learning over 3–4 months. — [Fool transcript](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2025/11/06/duolingo-duol-q3-2025-earnings-call-transcript)

### Inferences
- The decision rule is explicit. Ship experiments that improve every metric. When monetization and DAU conflict, DAU wins unless the DAU cost is negligible (the 25-vs-24 energy example). Free usage is capped by volume, not locked behind features, so the core learning stays free.
