# Gamified Learning Apps: Strengths and Pitfalls (Duolingo, Brilliant, Chess.com, Lichess, Babbel, Mimo, Speak, Elevate, Khan Academy)

Context: these notes are for a paid, 18+, gamified blackjack education site. Research was done 2026-11-24. Evidence quality: Duolingo has the most primary and press coverage. The smaller apps (Mimo, Elevate) are thinly covered. Much of the sentiment evidence is self-selected (Trustpilot, forums, Change.org) and shows what people complain about, not how often.

## 1. Duolingo: controversies, lessons, and what it does exceptionally well

### Takeaway
Duolingo's wins come from habit design: the streak, a culture of A/B testing everything, a distinct brand and character cast, and constant micro-celebration. Its 2025–2026 failures came from putting monetization and AI-cost framing ahead of how learners felt. The AI-first memo, the energy meter that limits even perfect play, and stacked event and chest screens between lessons all hit that nerve. By its Q4 2025 letter (Feb 2026), management said it was giving up more than $50M in bookings to remove free-tier friction because user growth had slowed.

### Cited Findings
**AI-first memo (Apr–Aug 2025)**
- Late April 2025: CEO Luis von Ahn's memo said Duolingo would "gradually stop using contractors to do work that AI can handle" and that headcount would only rise if a team could not automate more of its work — [The Register, 2025-04-29](https://www.theregister.com/2025/04/29/duolingo_ceo_ai_first_shift); [Entrepreneur](https://www.entrepreneur.com/business-news/duolingo-ceo-clarifies-ai-stance-after-backlash-read-the-memo/492141)
- May 2025: Fast Company reported a "massive social media backlash," and said the company had "not yet meaningfully addressed the policies" behind it — [Slashdot summarizing Fast Company, 2025-05-25](https://tech.slashdot.org/story/25/05/25/0347239/); [Fortune, 2025-05-24](https://fortune.com/2025/05/24/duolingo-ai-first-employees-ceo-luis-von-ahn)
- Aug 2025: von Ahn admitted the memo "did not give enough context." He said Duolingo had "never laid off any full-time employees," and that contractor numbers "have gone up and down depending on needs" — [Fortune, 2025-08-18](https://fortune.com/2025/08/18/duolingo-ceo-admits-controversial-ai-memo-did-not-give-enough-context-insists-company-never-laid-off-full-time-employees/)
- Sources disagree on whether the contractor policy was actually reversed. Staffing Industry reported a pullback on shifting work from contractors to AI, while Duolingo framed the episode as a communication problem — [Staffing Industry Analysts](https://www.staffingindustry.com/news/global-daily-news/duolingo-pulls-back-on-plans-to-shift-from-contractors-to-ai); [TechSpot](https://www.techspot.com/news/108054-duolingo-ceo-backtracks-ai-push-after-outcry-human.html)

**Energy system (tested from ~July 2025)**
- Energy replaced hearts for free users. Users get 25 energy, and every exercise drains it, correct or not. It refills over time or through a rewarded ad. Duolingo said the system "reward[s] learners for getting things right instead of penalizing" them, and that "learners do more in the Energy system than they were with Hearts" — [Duolingo blog](https://blog.duolingo.com/duolingo-energy/); [Duolingo Fandom wiki](https://duolingo.fandom.com/wiki/Energy)
- Backlash centered on skilled learners. Under hearts they could study for hours if they made no mistakes. Under energy, progress stops no matter how well they play. Users called it "the final straw," and Change.org petitions (one from Sept 2025) asked for hearts back — [Android Authority](https://www.androidauthority.com/quitting-duolingo-energy-system-3599842/); [Change.org](https://www.change.org/p/duolingo-hearts-not-energy); [TechIssuesToday](https://techissuestoday.com/duolingo-energy-system-user-backlash)
- A PCWorld writer complained that "Duolingo got easier when I paid for it," meaning that paying removed the challenge — [PCWorld](https://www.pcworld.com/article/2924235/duolingo-got-easier-when-i-paid-for-it-that-was-the-problem.html)

**Business consequences (Q4 2025 letter, published 2026-02-26)**
- Management said DAU growth was strong from 2022 to mid-2025 and then slowed. It attributed part of the slowdown to a heavier monetization push and guided 2026 DAU growth to about 20%. It estimated it is giving up more than $50M in bookings (about 5 points of growth) to remove friction from the free tier. 2026 guidance: bookings +10–12%, revenue +15–18%, adjusted EBITDA margin about 25% — [Duolingo Q4 2025 shareholder letter](https://investors.duolingo.com/static-files/961ce633-3cee-49d0-bd7a-2c63731d45fb) (the PDF returned 403 to direct fetch, so figures come via search summary); [Yahoo Finance Q4 call highlights](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/duolingo-q4-earnings-call-highlights-041400802.html)
- Shares slid after Duolingo said profits would slow as it pursued user growth — [Bloomberg, 2026-02-26](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-02-26/duolingo-slides-after-it-says-profits-to-slow-for-user-growth). Later coverage said the stock fell about 14% after Q1 2026 results and was down 40%+ YTD and about 80% from its peak — [Nasdaq/Motley Fool](https://www.nasdaq.com/articles/duolingo-stock-plummets-even-user-growth-soars-time-buy) (not verified against filings; one article's figures conflict on timing)
- Earlier precedent: in May 2024, "Duolingo sinks as user growth slows by most since 2022" — [Bloomberg, 2024-05-08](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2024-05-08/duolingo-boosts-revenue-outlook-as-daily-users-top-30-million)

**Course quality, events, and chests (2026)**
- A July 2026 Android Authority reader poll on course updates found "the clear majority" said the changes affected them negatively — [Android Authority poll](https://androidauthority.com/duolingo-course-updates-poll-results-3690803); see also [Android Authority, "Duolingo broken… why I quit"](https://www.androidauthority.com/duolingo-broken-updated-courses-why-i-quit-3686405/) and [course upgrade complaints](https://androidauthority.com/duolingo-course-upgrade-complaints-3685447)
- Several events, including treasure chests and avatar-suit events, can stack between lessons. After complaints that these screens "interfere with learning," a Duolingo rep said on r/duolingo the company would test letting users opt out of them (article date not confirmed, 2026) — [Android Authority](https://androidauthority.com/duolingo-event-screen-opt-out-3737299)
- Early Bird and Night Owl chests disappeared without explanation, which drew complaints (undated) — [TechIssuesToday](https://techissuestoday.com/duolingo-xp-boost-and-daily-chests-changed)
- A third-party tracker reported a spike in negative App Store reviews in June 2026 (methodology unverified) — [Unstar](https://unstar.app/events/duolingo-language-chess-complaints-june-2026)
- Trustpilot reviews from late 2025 to early 2026 describe users switching to Babbel and LingQ — [TechIssuesToday](https://techissuestoday.com/duolingo-energy-system-user-backlash); [Trustpilot](https://uk.trustpilot.com/review/duolingo.com?page=2)

**Strengths: habit design and A/B testing**
- "We A/B test basically every change we make." Learners who reach a 7-day streak are 2.4x more likely to return the next day. Users can now equip two Streak Freezes so a missed day doesn't end the habit — [Duolingo blog, "How the streak builds habit"](https://blog.duolingo.com/how-duolingo-streak-builds-habit); [Improving the streak](https://blog.duolingo.com/improving-the-streak)
- Changing the button copy from "continue" to "commit to my goal" "significantly increased retention." The first 7 days are the most fragile period — [Lenny's Newsletter, Behind the product: Duolingo streaks](https://lennysnewsletter.com/p/behind-the-product-duolingo-streaks)
- The growth team always runs at least five A/B tests at once — [First Round Review](https://review.firstround.com/the-tenets-of-a-b-testing-from-duolingos-master-growth-hacker)
- Counterpoint: habit research cited in Mobbin's study of 859 streak designs found that missing a single day "did not materially affect the habit formation process." Strict streak punishment is a retention tactic, not a learning need — [Mobbin](https://mobbin.com/blog/streaks-are-about-losing-a-study-of-859-streak-designs)

**Strengths: brand, social, and character cast**
- In the Feb 2025 "Dead Duo" stunt (the owl was "killed" by a Cybertruck), the account reached 1.7B impressions in two weeks, a record — [Fast Company](https://www.fastcompany.com/91313082/duolingo-dead-duo-owl-social-media-campaign). Daily mentions rose from about 11k to about 59.9k — [Meltwater](https://www.meltwater.com/en/blog/duolingo-dead-mascot-campaign); [TechCrunch, 2025-02-18](https://techcrunch.com/2025/02/18/duolingo-killed-its-mascot-with-a-cybertruck-and-its-going-weirdly-well)
- Duolingo credited "unhinged and viral marketing" (a 5-second Super Bowl ad, Duolingo on Ice, a Netflix tie-in) with supporting growth. It reported DAU +51% and revenue +41% in Q4 2024, though the stunt's own effect was not isolated — [Business Insider NL](https://www.businessinsider.nl/duolingo-killed-its-cartoon-owl-mascot-for-another-unhinged-marketing-stunt-except-in-one-country/); risk framing from [NPR, 2025-02-26](https://npr.org/2025/02/26/nx-s1-5309785/duolingo-owl-mascot-lives); "engagement and criticism" from [MediaPost](https://www.mediapost.com/publications/article/403787/duolingos-mascot-killing-stunt-generated-engageme.html)
- The 2025 backlash showed the limit of a beloved brand voice. Fast Company noted that the AI-first backlash was strong enough that the social team went quiet — [Slashdot/Fast Company](https://tech.slashdot.org/story/25/05/25/0347239/)

### Inferences
- For a paid blackjack site, the energy lesson applies directly. Never cap or meter play in a way that punishes good performance, and never make paying the way to remove challenge. Gate content, not effort.
- Interstitial event and chest screens that delay the next hand will be read as "monetization over learning." Users can't skip them, so they hit hardest for paid users.
- Streaks work but get resented. Offer forgiveness (freezes, grace days), because research suggests a missed day doesn't break a habit.
- A strong mascot voice is an asset, but only while the product's behavior matches the brand's warmth.

### Gaps
- I could not open the Q4 2025 or Q1–Q3 2026 shareholder letters directly (403). Figures come via search summaries and press.
- I found no primary data on energy-system retention, or on whether Duolingo reverted the change in 2026.
- I found no direct Reddit threads on "stacked events/chests in late 2026." The evidence is secondhand (Android Authority) and the exact dates are unconfirmed.
- Notification annoyance: I found no 2024–2026 citable source in this pass. The "passive-aggressive Duo" notifications are well known but not documented here.

## 2. Chess.com vs Lichess: monetization, preference, and growth surges

### Takeaway
Chess.com runs a freemium model. Free users see ads and get one Game Review per day, and Platinum and Diamond tiers unlock unlimited review, explanations, and Insights. Lichess gives everything away free, with no ads, funded by donations. Players credit Lichess with free unlimited analysis and Chess.com with polish, community, and social features. Chess.com's early-2023 surge broke its servers, and it reached about 150M members by Oct 2023.

### Cited Findings
- Free Chess.com members get one Game Review per day, with the same cap on explanations — [Chess.com features guide](https://www.chess.com/article/view/chesscom-features); [USCF Sales](https://www.uscfsales.com/blogs/chess-technology/lichess-vs-chess-com)
- After the Sept 1 plan changes, unlimited Game Review is limited to Platinum and Diamond. Diamond adds unlimited review plus explanations and Insights — [Chess.com support: plan changes](https://support.chess.com/en/articles/8609584-september-1st-plan-changes); [Premium plans](https://support.chess.com/en/articles/8562418-what-are-the-differences-between-membership-types); [Prices changing for new members](https://www.chess.com/article/view/prices-changing)
- Paywall resentment: forum threads include "You just gutted my way of improving and put it behind a paywall," and an r/chess thread from July 2024 titled "another free feature locked behind a pay wall" — [Chess.com forum](https://www.chess.com/forum/view/game-analysis/you-just-gutted-my-way-of-improving-and-put-it-behind-a-paywall-thanks-for-nothing); [r/chess mirror, 2024-07-16](https://lr.us.psf.lt/r/chess/comments/1e4ywkb/another_free_feature_locked_behind_a_pay_wall_on)
- Lichess is preferred for "unlimited free game analysis, unlimited puzzles," no ads, and a nonprofit model. Chess.com is credited with a bigger community, clubs, streaming, and a polished UX, and its ads between games are called "very distracting" — [Lichess forum: Lichess vs chess.com](https://lichess.org/forum/lichess-feedback/lichess-vs-chesscom); [Chess.com forum: why lichess > chess.com](https://www.chess.com/forum/view/off-topic/why-lichess-chess-com); [Chess.com blog, "Why the Paid Giant Outplays the Free Rival"](https://www.chess.com/blog/preptonthechessmaster/chess-com-vs-lichess-why-the-paid-giant-outplays-the-free-rival)
- Lichess's 2023 review: growing donations let it hire a full-time mobile developer for a new app, and it passed 5B rated games — [Lichess blog](https://lichess.org/blog/ZZQVPhAAACEAw2ZR/-)
- The surge: Chess.com hit 100M members on 2022-12-16. On 2023-01-20 it had 10M daily active members and 31.7M games played. 8.9M new registrations came in Jan–Feb 2023, with a record 403k signups on Feb 3. It bought about $2M of hardware. "Honestly, this sucks," it said of the outages — [Chess.com blog, "Chess is booming and our servers are struggling"](https://www.chess.com/blog/CHESScom/chess-is-booming-and-our-servers-are-struggling); [Chess.com January 2023 update](https://chess.com/article/view/chesscom-update-january-2023); [PC Gamer](https://pcgamer.com/honestly-this-sucks-says-chesscom-as-its-servers-cant-keep-up-with-chesss-explosive-popularity); [Wikipedia](https://Www.Wikipedia.org/wiki/Chess.com)
- The Jan 2023 surge was linked to the "Mittens" AI bot, a personality-driven bot that went viral — [Yahoo/Malaysia](https://malaysia.news.yahoo.com/psycho-ai-bot-crashes-chess-121832408.html)

### Inferences
- Game Review is Chess.com's best-converting paywall because it ties into a "how did I do / what did I miss" moment right after the user plays. For blackjack, a per-session "hand review" (each decision versus basic strategy, with EV lost) is the natural equivalent. One free review per day mirrors Chess.com's proven pattern.
- Lichess shows that a free competitor will exist, which for blackjack means free strategy charts and trainers. A paid site has to beat free on polish, personality, progression, and community, not on information alone.
- Personality bots like Mittens drove viral surges, which suggests characterful AI dealers or opponents could be a growth lever.

### Gaps
- I found no published Chess.com retention or cohort data after the 2023 surge. "How they kept users" is not documented beyond membership counts.
- Lichess donation totals for 2024–2026 were not found in this pass.
- Current (2026) Chess.com tier prices were not verified.

## 3. Brilliant, Mimo, Elevate, Speak, Babbel: complaints and praise

### Takeaway
Across these subscription apps, the main complaint is about billing, not content. People report auto-renewal surprises, no refunds, trials that require a card, and confusion about cancelling through the App Store. Content praise is strong, especially Brilliant's interactivity and Babbel's structure, while repetition and shallow depth for advanced users are the main content complaints.

### Cited Findings
- Brilliant (Trustpilot, about 2025–2026): reviewers report surprise renewals ("keeps renewing… without telling them"), refused refunds, and confusion over cancelling in the app. Brilliant replies that its terms "do not allow for refunds" and that reminders are sent in advance. Trustpilot's summary says reviewers praise the content but complain about renewals and the clarity of billing — [Trustpilot: Brilliant](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/brilliant.org?page=6); [UK](https://uk.trustpilot.com/review/brilliant.org?page=3); [AU](https://au.trustpilot.com/review/brilliant.org?page=3)
- Babbel: trial billing is a recurring complaint. One quoted case describes a 7-day trial that required payment and turned into an annual charge. Repetitive content is listed as the second most common complaint. Babbel's Trustpilot replies point users to App Store or Play cancellation, since deleting the app doesn't stop billing. Its overall Trustpilot score is about 4.4–4.5, with praise for its structure and speaking content — [Unstar 2026 review analysis](https://unstar.app/blog/language-learning-app-reviews-duolingo-babbel-rosetta-stone-2026) (methodology unverified); [Trustpilot: Babbel](https://uk.trustpilot.com/review/babbel.com?page=3)
- Speak (2026): Premium costs about $17.99/mo or $83.99/yr, and Premium Plus about $39.99/mo or $164.99/yr. Reviewers report mispronunciations or reversed word order still scored as correct, and feedback too shallow for advanced learners. The price isn't shown until you reach the in-app paywall — [Apple App Store: Speak](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/speak-learn-english/id1286609883); [LanguaTalk, 2026](https://languatalk.com/blog/speak-app-review/); [SpeakShark, 2026](https://speakshark.com/blog/speak-app-review-2026)
- Independent efficacy evidence for Babbel: a 2020 Loewen et al. study of 54 university students found gains in oral proficiency, grammar, and vocabulary — [Duolingo/Hawaii ScholarSpace context listing](https://scholarspace.manoa.hawaii.edu/items/140c6a0c-0035-4c87-b678-a0d74dd53de3) (found via search summary; the primary paper was not fetched)

### Inferences
- For an 18+ paid site, transparent billing is a differentiator. That means showing the price before asking for a card, sending a renewal reminder, one-click web cancellation, and a stated refund window. It's especially important because a gambling-adjacent product already starts with low trust.
- AI grading that marks wrong answers as correct (Speak) damages credibility. Blackjack has exact correct answers (basic strategy, counts), so grading must be strict and explained.

### Gaps
- I found no reliable 2024–2026 sources on Mimo or Elevate complaints or praise. Both need a dedicated App Store or Reddit pass.
- I found no Brilliant-specific praise beyond Trustpilot's summary, such as notes on its interactive visuals. Claims about its "interactive problems" are general knowledge and not cited here.

## 4. Mastery and learning outcomes: do gamified apps build real skill, and how do they prove it?

### Takeaway
There is evidence of real but modest gains, and most of it comes from the companies themselves. Duolingo's 2024 studies (staff-authored) show proficiency gains in reading and listening, plus all four skills in a small Spanish study. Independent work found weaker oral gains. Khan Academy's strongest evidence ties skills mastered, not minutes spent, to test gains. The most credible apps prove progress with external benchmarks such as ACTFL/CEFR levels, state tests, or Elo ratings.

### Cited Findings
- Smith, Jiang & Peters (2024), *Language Learning & Technology*: n=48 university students using Duolingo Spanish for 3 months improved in reading, writing, listening, and speaking (Duolingo-affiliated authors) — [Duolingo blog: efficacy results](https://blog.duolingo.com/results-duolingo-efficacy-studies); [Cambridge core reader](https://www.cambridge.org/core/product/68C0E7E296669798089C84CDC7F3BB9E/core-reader)
- Jiang, Peters, Plonsky & Pajak (2024), *CALICO Journal*: Duolingo English learners gained an additional ACTFL sublevel in reading and listening (the study didn't cover speaking) — [CALICO](https://journal.equinoxpub.com/Calico/article/view/26704); [NAU](https://experts.nau.edu/en/publications/the-effectiveness-of-duolingo-english-courses-in-developing-readi/)
- Independent counterpoint: Loewen et al. (2019), on beginner Turkish learners using Duolingo, found better written than oral development — [ScholarSpace](https://scholarspace.manoa.hawaii.edu/items/140c6a0c-0035-4c87-b678-a0d74dd53de3/full)
- Khan Academy: it recommends 30+ min/week, and Newark compliance rose from about 50% (2022–23) to 70%+ (2023–24). In Newark, each 10 extra skills mastered was worth about +1 point on the PARCC state test, while courses contain 120–140 skills — [Newark white paper, Nov 2025](https://blog.khanacademy.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Newark_White_Paper-2.pdf); [Khan blog, 3-year efficacy](https://blog.khanacademy.org/?p=19958)
- Khan Academy uses "skills to proficient" as its outcome metric: 60+ skills per year marks a "Yearly Proficient Learner" — [Khan efficacy results, Nov 2024](https://blog.khanacademy.org/?p=18194); [skills to proficient](https://blog.khanacademy.org/?p=13201)
- PNAS study (~200k students, 2021–23): measuring skills mastered rather than time spent spread benefits more evenly across students. A co-author sits on Khan's research board — [Stanford News, Jan 2026](https://news.stanford.edu/stories/2026/01/online-mastery-math-practice-software-educational-impact-research)
- An RCT summarized by Khan (Oreopoulos et al., 2024) found gains of 0.12–0.22 SD from a year-long mastery intervention (the paper was not verified) — [Khan blog](https://blog.khanacademy.org/?p=14445); [NBER paper](https://conference.nber.org/conf_papers/f197503.pdf)
- Chess platforms prove progress with ratings (Elo/Glicko) and per-game accuracy from Game Review — [Chess.com features](https://www.chess.com/article/view/chesscom-features)

### Inferences
- XP and streaks measure engagement, not mastery. The fluency complaints Duolingo gets are what happens when the visible score is engagement. A blackjack site should make its headline progress number skill-based: decision accuracy against basic strategy, a count-accuracy rating, or an Elo-like "table rating." It should test that number on fresh, timed hands, not on repeated drills.
- Following Khan, count "skills mastered" (for example, soft hands, splits, deviations, true count conversion) and require spaced re-checks before marking a skill mastered.
- Certificates or level tests tied to an objective standard, like a timed casino-condition test, are more credible than badges.

### Gaps
- I found no truly independent, large-scale RCT of Duolingo from 2024–2026.
- I found no studies on whether gamified trainers for card games or blackjack transfer to real-table accuracy.

## 5. Delight and feel: micro-interactions, sound, characters, and avoiding repetition

### Takeaway
Duolingo treats delight as part of the core product. Its design system centers on "micro-successes," every action gets feedback, the correct-answer chime doubles as a brand cue, and Rive-animated characters lip-sync in lessons (with an AI video call featuring Lily). Public writeups cover the techniques better than they prove the impact, and I found no rigorous study of the effect of celebration variety.

### Cited Findings
- Duolingo's design voice guide says Duolingo is built on micro-successes, and that it makes steps clear, celebrates learners when they succeed, and encourages them when they stumble — [design.duolingo.com](https://design.duolingo.com/writing/voice)
- Duolingo's character viseme work aimed to make characters "lively, engaging study buddies." The post acknowledges that animating mouth movements was a large job — [Duolingo blog: world character visemes](https://blog.duolingo.com/world-character-visemes)
- Rive case study: Duolingo turned Lily, its "most unimpressed character," into an AI-powered video-call partner, and hired creative technologists for its Rive work — [Rive blog](https://rive.app/blog/duolingo-s-ai-powered-video-call-brings-lily-to-life)
- Third-party descriptions of the pipeline (unverified): speech is mapped to phonemes and then visemes, timing is sent to a Rive state machine (idle, speaking, reacting), and each character has about 20 mouth shapes — [DEV Community](https://dev.to/uianimation/how-duolingo-uses-rive-for-their-character-animation-and-how-you-can-build-a-similar-rive-mascot-5d19). An independent build used 10 visemes and a single state-machine input, and found the hard part was getting timing data from TTS — [Contra, Michał Stencel](https://contra.com/p/AAQksb6B-lip-sync-animation)
- Sound as UX: the correct-answer chime becomes a progress signal and reappears in push notifications. The author calls this "sound design used as UX strategy, not decoration" — [The Cognitive Load Substack](https://thecognitiveload.substack.com/p/a-40-day-duolingo-streak)
- Feedback on every action, such as an animated speaker icon during pronunciation — [Medium, Dissecting Duolingo's Design](https://medium.com/@assenavseolb/dissecting-duolingos-design-f1113a1db8cd)
- Duolingo's streak milestone animation post covers its motion principles (contents only partly retrieved) — [Duolingo blog](https://blog.duolingo.com/streak-milestone-design-animation)
- Its director of product design says consumer rewards can look like "confetti, coins, or sound effects" — [Amplitude](https://www.amplitude.com/blog/next-gen-builders-yudi-sun)
- Negative side of feel: stacked post-lesson celebration and event screens drew complaints that they "interfere with learning," and Duolingo is testing an opt-out — [Android Authority](https://androidauthority.com/duolingo-event-screen-opt-out-3737299)

### Inferences
- Keep in-flow feedback under about a second (chip sounds, card flips, a dealer reaction) and save full-screen celebrations for real milestones, like mastering a skill or a rating jump. Let users skip or turn them off.
- Vary reactions with pools of character lines and animations, weighted by context such as streaks, comebacks, and near-misses, so feedback doesn't get repetitive. A Rive state machine with a few inputs is the standard way to drive this on the web.
- For an 18+ blackjack product, celebration design must reward correct decisions, not wins. Slot-machine-style win celebrations would teach the wrong lesson and create responsible-gambling risk. This is an inference; no source covers it directly.

### Gaps
- I found no academic or primary 2024–2026 study that quantifies the retention or learning effect of celebration variety, haptics, or sound design in learning apps.
- I found no Duolingo primary source on haptics.
- The full Duolingo engineering breakdown of lip-sync was not retrieved. Pipeline details come from third-party, partly promotional writeups.
