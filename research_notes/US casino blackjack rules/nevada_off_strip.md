# Nevada Off-Strip Casino Blackjack Rules (Downtown LV, LV locals, Henderson, Laughlin, Reno/Sparks, Lake Tahoe)

Research date: 2026-11-24. 40 casino records. Accuracy over coverage: every field I could not tie to a dated source says "unverified".

## Key Question 1: Which sources are current and reliable for off-Strip Nevada blackjack rules (2025-2026)?

### Takeaway
Las Vegas off-Strip and Laughlin have good dated surveys from 2025-2026. Reno, Sparks and Lake Tahoe do not: the only full rule charts are undated and partly stale, and the newest source (casinoedge, checked Oct 2026) confirms only payout and deck count for a handful of Reno casinos. Treat Reno and Tahoe rule details as low confidence.

### Cited Findings
- **Wizard of Vegas Las Vegas Blackjack Survey.** The main Las Vegas table is dated January 2025. The page shows "Updated Nov 20, 2026", with a small 2026 section (checked Nov 21, 2026) re-checking only Ellis Island, El Cortez, Downtown Grand and Golden Nugget. It covers only 3:2 games and lists table counts, min/max, decks, H17/S17, DAS, late surrender, RSA, house edge and procedure notes (cut card, notched shoe, no mid-shoe entry, "database participant"). No row restricts doubling, so doubling on any two cards is implied. — [Wizard of Vegas survey](https://wizardofvegas.com/guides/blackjack-survey/)
- **Vegas Advantage Downtown survey.** Last updated January 25, 2026. It includes 6:5 games, side-bet games and video games. It says all downtown blackjack is H17 except Circa's high-limit 6-deck game (S17). — [Vegas Advantage Downtown 2026](https://vegasadvantage.com/las-vegas-blackjack/downtown-las-vegas-blackjack-survey-2021/)
- **CasinoReports downtown trip diary.** Updated October 8, 2026. It records a summer Saturday visit with no year stated, and gives minimums, payouts and continuous-shuffler (CSM) sightings for 10 downtown casinos. — [CasinoReports](https://www.casinoreports.com/news/downtown-vegas-casino-trip-diary/)
- **US Casino Advantage Laughlin survey.** Visited April 2026, page updated September 16, 2026. It gives min/max, decks, payout and rules by casino. Its general default is H17, double any two cards, no DAS, no surrender, no RSA unless noted. — [US Casino Advantage Laughlin](https://uscasinoadvantage.com/gambling/nevada/laughlin/)
- **Easy Money Blackjack charts (Reno, "Other Cities").** Both are titled 2026 and carry a ©2026 notice, but neither shows a table update date. Reader comments run from 2013 to 2023. The Reno chart still lists closed or renamed properties (Siena, Harrah's Reno, Sands Regency), so it is stale. — [EMB Reno](https://easymoneyblackjack.com/reno-nv-blackjack-rules-conditions/); [EMB Other Cities](https://easymoneyblackjack.com/other-cities-nv-blackjack-rules-conditions/)
- **casinoedge.app Reno/Tahoe map.** Checked 2026-10-30. It says table rules are confirmed for only 5 of 24 casinos: Atlantis, Eldorado, J Resort, Peppermill and Silver Legacy. It uses current property names: Caesars Republic Lake Tahoe, Golden Nugget Lake Tahoe, Bally's Lake Tahoe and J Resort. — [casinoedge Reno-Tahoe](https://casinoedge.app/en/map/united-states/nevada/reno-tahoe/)
- **Statewide pattern.** Las Vegas Advisor says most Reno casinos hit soft 17 and restrict doubling to 10/11. It also says higher-limit Reno casinos tend to deal 6:5 single deck while lower-limit casinos keep 3:2. — [LVA: Nevada blackjack rules](https://www.lasvegasadvisor.com/question/nevada-blackjack-rules/)

### Inferences
- In this region, H17 is the default almost everywhere. S17 shows up mainly in high-limit 6-deck games: Station Casinos high-limit pits (Red Rock, Durango, GVR, Palace), M Resort, Circa high-limit, and the Plaza $25 game.
- Among Las Vegas locals casinos, the typical low-limit "good" game is 3:2 double deck, H17, DAS, no surrender, no RSA (about 0.40% edge). South Point and El Cortez low-limit double deck deny DAS (about 0.53%).
- Downtown has shifted toward 6:5 and side-bet games at low minimums (Four Queens, Binion's, Fremont low tables, Golden Nugget under $15). 3:2 generally starts at $15-$25.

### Gaps
- Surrender, RSA and CSM are rarely reported for Reno, Sparks and Tahoe. Those fields are "unverified".
- Several sources were read through a summarizing fetch tool. Exact table cells (especially Wizard double-rule columns) should be spot-checked against the source page before shipping.
- Atlantis's official blackjack page returned HTTP 403. A search snippet suggests it advertises 3:2 single and double deck while its posted rules text says 6:5, so it is unresolved. — [Atlantis BJ page](https://atlantiscasino.com/casino/table-games/black-jack)

## Key Question 2: Casino-by-casino blackjack game variants and rules

### Takeaway
The table below has 40 records: 12 Downtown LV, 13 LV/Henderson locals, 6 Laughlin, 6 Reno/Sparks and 3 Lake Tahoe. How much to trust each group depends on its source. - **Coordinates, ZIPs and websites.** These come from my general knowledge of the official addresses. I did not fetch them per casino this session. Coordinates are approximate to roughly 100-300 m. Avi and J Resort coordinates are rough, and J Resort's address and website are unverified. Spot-check against OpenStreetMap or Wikipedia before publishing.
