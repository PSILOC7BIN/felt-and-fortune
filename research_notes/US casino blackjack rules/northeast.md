# Northeast US Casino Blackjack Rules (NJ/AC, CT, PA, NY, MA, MD, DE)

Research date: 2026-11-24. Budget-limited pass (about 15 tool calls). **Read this first:** I found **no rule survey dated 2025 or 2026** for any Northeast state. The best structured sources are the US Casino Advantage / Atlantic City Advantage surveys (site visits in May 2021, pages last updated September 2022). Forum trip reports are mostly from 2018–2019. The only 2026-current rule pages I could confirm are two official casino pages (Rivers Philadelphia and Turning Stone). Every rule below carries its source date. Treat anything before 2025 as **stale and needing a check** before an app shows it as current.

## Q1: What sources exist, and how current and reliable are they?

### Takeaway
No dated 2025–2026 rule survey exists in public sources for Northeast casinos. Some sites carry a "2026" title, but their data is far older (one lists casinos that closed in 2014). Official casino rule pages are current but usually leave out deck counts and limits.

### Cited Findings
- The Atlantic City Advantage survey comes from a "May 2021 visit" and the page was "Last updated: September 4, 2022". It says all AC games allow double after split (DAS) and all AC Spanish 21 stands on soft 17. Its own Harrah's Spanish 21 entry says the dealer hits, which contradicts that. — [Atlantic City Advantage](https://atlanticcityadvantage.com/blackjack/)
- The Easy Money Blackjack page is titled "Atlantic City … Chart 2026" but gives no update date. It lists Revel, Showboat, Trump Plaza, Trump Taj Mahal and Atlantic Club, all closed in 2014–2016, and it does not list Hard Rock or Ocean. **Its data is obsolete despite the title. Do not use it.** — [Easy Money Blackjack](https://easymoneyblackjack.com/atlantic-city-blackjack-rules-conditions/)
- The Pennsylvania survey says "In May 2021, we visited fourteen Pennsylvania casinos". It reports that PA's 6- and 8-deck games must pay 3:2, stand on soft 17, offer surrender and allow doubling before and after splitting. Each casino sets its own resplit rules. — [US Casino Advantage PA](https://uscasinoadvantage.com/blackjack/pennsylvania/)
- The Maryland survey's visits were in 2021, and the page says it was "accurate as of September 4, 2022". — [US Casino Advantage MD](https://uscasinoadvantage.com/blackjack/maryland/)
- The US Casino Advantage index (updated 2024-11-09) has **no** blackjack surveys for CT, NY, MA or DE. — [US Casino Advantage index](https://uscasinoadvantage.com/blackjack/)
- A 2026 guide on toolsgambling.com claims Borgata and Hard Rock run 6-deck S17 games with DAS and surrender at $25, and Ocean runs 8-deck S17 at $15. Its comparison table is garbled, it cites no survey, and its claim that AC dealers always stand on soft 17 contradicts the dated surveys. **Treated as unreliable.** — [toolsgambling](https://toolsgambling.com/blog/atlantic-city-blackjack-rules)
- Counting Edge cautions that AC floors mix H17 and S17 games, so the rule must be checked table by table. — [Counting Edge](https://www.countingedge.com/atlantic-city-blackjack/)

### Inferences
- In the Northeast, the dated evidence shows a fairly stable two-tier pattern. Low-limit tables are 8-deck H17 and high-limit or $25+ tables are 6-deck S17. That holds in AC, CT and MD. Pennsylvania is the exception because state rules require S17. The app could present this pattern as a "typical" profile, but each casino's record still needs re-verification.

### Gaps
- There is no 2025–2026 survey for any state. Current minimums are especially stale, since post-2021 inflation of table minimums is widely reported but I did not find it documented with a source.
- No source reported max bets or continuous shuffler (CSM) status for most casinos.

## Q2: Rules by state (cited)

### Takeaway
Rules are at least partly documented for about 30 of the 38 casinos. Most of that data is from 2018–2022. Eight casinos have no rule source at all: del Lago, Seneca Niagara, Rivers Schenectady, Bally's Dover, Harrington, Hollywood Penn National's 6-deck game, Live! Pittsburgh's current game, and MGM Springfield's verified rules. Those are marked "unverified".

### Cited Findings
**New Jersey / Atlantic City**
- **Bally's:** $25 8-deck H17 with surrender, aces resplit twice and no mid-shoe entry. $100 6-deck S17 in high limit. $10 Spanish 21 S17. (May 2021) — [ACA](https://atlanticcityadvantage.com/blackjack/)
- **Borgata:** $10 8-deck H17 ("dozens of tables"), $25 6-deck S17 outside the high-limit salon, $100 high limit, $10 Spanish 21 S17. (May 2021) — [ACA](https://atlanticcityadvantage.com/blackjack/)
- **Caesars:** $15 8-deck game (soft-17 rule not stated) and $100 6-deck S17 high limit, sometimes $50 when slow. $15 Spanish 21 S17. (May 2021) — [ACA](https://atlanticcityadvantage.com/blackjack/)
- **Golden Nugget:** $15 8-deck H17 with aces resplit once, $25 8-deck S17, $50 6-deck high limit with surrender, $15 Spanish 21. (May 2021) — [ACA](https://atlanticcityadvantage.com/blackjack/)
- **Hard Rock:** $15 8-deck H17 with RSA and surrender, $25 8-deck S17, $100 6-deck high limit with RSA and surrender, $15 Spanish 21. (May 2021) — [ACA](https://atlanticcityadvantage.com/blackjack/)
- **Harrah's:** $10–15 8-deck H17 and $100 6-deck S17 high limit with aces resplit once. Its 6:5 tables were closed during the visit. Spanish 21 is $15, and the page conflicts on whether it is H17 or S17. (May 2021) — [ACA](https://atlanticcityadvantage.com/blackjack/)
- **Ocean:** 8-deck H17 with aces resplit once and surrender (minimum not stated). $25 6-deck S17. $100 6-deck S17 high limit with the same rules. $15 Spanish 21. (May 2021) — [ACA](https://atlanticcityadvantage.com/blackjack/)
- **Resorts:** $15 8-deck game, S17 in one pit and H17 in the other, aces resplit once. $50 high limit S17. $15 Spanish 21. (May 2021) — [ACA](https://atlanticcityadvantage.com/blackjack/)
- **Tropicana:** $10–15 8-deck H17, $50 6-deck S17, $10–15 Spanish 21. (May 2021) — [ACA](https://atlanticcityadvantage.com/blackjack/)
- An undated, older minimums page lists $15 weekday and $25 weekend minimums at Borgata and Hard Rock. — [actableminimums.com](https://actableminimums.com/blackjack-atlantic-city/)

**Connecticut** (forum, July 2018)
- **Foxwoods:** 8-deck 3:2 H17 with DAS and late surrender, plus 6-deck S17 in high limit. All games paid 3:2, and dealers cut about 1–1.25 decks. — [Blackjack Forum thread, 2018-07-24 to 07-29](https://www.blackjacktheforum.com/showthread.php?41054-Connecticut-Native-American-Casino-Report&p=254341)
- **Mohegan Sun:** moved from 6 decks to 8. All games H17 except S17 at $50+ high limit. Late surrender and DAS. A 2018 report says the $10 tables went to 6:5. — [Blackjack Forum thread](https://www.blackjacktheforum.com/showthread.php?41054-Connecticut-Native-American-Casino-Report&p=254341); [6:5 thread](https://www.blackjacktheforum.com/showthread.php?40828-Mohegan-Sun-Ct-goes-6-5=&40828-Mohegan-Sun-Ct-goes-6-5=&p=251390#post251390)
- A forum post says 10s can be split at Foxwoods but not at Mohegan Sun (undated). — [Wizard of Vegas](https://wizardofvegas.com/forum/gambling/blackjack/22658-mohegan-sun-vs-foxwoods/)

The page describes surrender as "early surrender" for half the wager, "only if the dealer doesn't have blackjack" (that description matches late surrender, so the wording is inconsistent). - **Parx:** $10–15 6- and 8-deck games and Spanish 21. **Harrah's Philadelphia:** $15 6/8-deck, $100 6-deck high limit, Spanish 21. **Live! **New York**
- **Turning Stone (official page, current: lists November tournaments):** 3:2, the dealer hits soft 17 ("must draw to 16 and soft 17"). Double on the first two cards. DAS except on double-deck tables. Pairs resplit to 4 hands. Split aces get one card each and **cannot be resplit**. **Surrender is not offered in standard blackjack**, only late surrender in Spanish 21. Double-deck tables exist, and the shoe deck count is not stated. — [Turning Stone](https://www.turningstone.com/gaming/table-games). A search-result snippet implied surrender in regular blackjack, but the fetched page text limits it to Spanish 21.
- **Resorts World Catskills (about 2018–2019 forums):** 6-deck H17 on the main floor and S17 in high limit, 3:2 at all tables, surrender offered on request. The opening-day report differs and mentions an 8-deck H17 game. — [Blackjack Forum](https://www.blackjacktheforum.com/showthread.php?40289-Trip-Report-Resorts-Catskills=&p=248650#post248650); [Wizard of Vegas](https://wizardofvegas.com/forum/gambling/blackjack/36026-rw-catskills-bj/); [bj21](https://bj21.com/boards/free/sub_boards/free/topics/need-bj-report-for-resorts-world-catskills-which-opened-today)
- **del Lago, Seneca Niagara, Rivers Schenectady:** I found no rule source.

The Gaming Commission allows 6:5 if the odds are posted. — [Gaming America](https://gamingamerica.com/news/2103/massachusetts-court-rules-in-favor-of-65-blackjack-odds); [CommonWealth Beacon](https://commonwealthbeacon.org/uncategorized/casinos-hoping-odds-in-sjc-case-tilt-in-their-favor/)
- **Encore (older forum post, about 2019):** 8-deck H17 with late surrender and no RSA. 22 tables paid 6:5 and 17 paid 3:2, with $25 and $50 minimums. — [Wizard of Vegas](https://wizardofvegas.com/forum/gambling/blackjack/33327-encore-boston-harbor-sued-over-6-5-bj/); [Blackjack Forum](https://www.blackjacktheforum.com/showthread.php?42361-Encore-Boston=&42361-Encore-Boston=&p=270500#post270500)
- **MGM Springfield:** a commercial guide lists a $25 6-deck S17 game with DAS, surrender and RSA, from an uncited source. **Unverified.** — [legalblackjack.com](https://www.legalblackjack.com/article/blackjack-in-boston/)

**Maryland** (2021 visits, "accurate as of 2022-09-04")
- **MGM National Harbor:** $15 6-deck 6:5 H17 with RSA and CSM. $25 6-deck 3:2 H17 with surrender and RSA, CSM at some tables. $50 6-deck H17. $200 6-deck 3:2 S17 high limit. Pontoon. — [USCA MD](https://uscasinoadvantage.com/blackjack/maryland/)
- **Live! MD:** $15 6:5 H17 CSM. $25 8-deck 3:2 H17 with surrender and RSA. $100 6-deck 3:2 S17 with surrender and RSA. $25 Spanish 21. — [USCA MD](https://uscasinoadvantage.com/blackjack/maryland/)
- **Horseshoe Baltimore:** $10 6:5 H17 CSM. $50 6-deck 3:2 H17 with surrender. $100 6-deck 3:2 S17 with surrender. — [USCA MD](https://uscasinoadvantage.com/blackjack/maryland/)
- **Hollywood Perryville:** $15 8-deck 3:2 H17 with surrender. $50 6-deck 3:2 S17 with surrender. $15 Spanish 21. — [USCA MD](https://uscasinoadvantage.com/blackjack/maryland/)

**Delaware**
- **Delaware Park:** 6 decks, S17, 3:2, per a forum post from about 2015. — [Wizard of Vegas](https://wizardofvegas.com/forum/questions-and-answers/math/2352-what-is-the-house-advantage-at-delaware-park-casino-on-there-black-jack-game/)
- One unsourced guide claims 6:5 at lower limits at Bally's Dover and Delaware Park during peak hours. **Unverified.** — [theexceptionalpet.com](https://theexceptionalpet.com/delaware-casino-table-games/)

### Inferences
- Maryland's low-limit games ($10–15) at the big three casinos used 6:5 and CSMs as of 2021–22. For app users, Maryland is the clearest "avoid low-limit tables" example.
- Massachusetts allows 6:5 by regulation. Encore's mix of 6:5 and 3:2 tables makes it the second example.

### Gaps
- Max bets were not found for any casino.
- CSM status is documented only for Maryland.
- There is no 2025–2026 confirmation that the AC 8-deck H17 low-limit tables still exist at the stated minimums.
