# Earn Ace's wardrobe through accurate play

Cosmetics in learning apps and card games pay off mainly as **engagement and subscription glue, not as a revenue line**. Duolingo's in-app purchases fell to **2.2% of revenue** in Q3 2026, yet its first cosmetic collection event was "one of our biggest experiment wins for getting learners to complete more lessons." Across card games, card backs are the default cosmetic. They are earned at least as often as sold, and paid backs cost only $1–3. Character skins anchor the top of every price ladder, and rarity maps directly to fixed prices. For this app that points to four rules. First, price a catalog of about 40 launch items in Stars on a four-step ladder (**150 / 400 / 900 / 1,800**), so a committed learner earns a Legendary Ace skin in about three weeks. Second, release items in themed table sets of card back, felt, Ace skin and avatar item. Third, make Pro's cosmetic perks additive (a monthly Pro card back, animated felt variants, loadout slots) rather than a Star multiplier, so Stars stay strictly earned. Fourth, add a Quests tab built on the industry's modal structure: **3 dailies (Easy/Medium/Hard), a weekly milestone track, and one monthly event**. The rewards go to accuracy and spaced review, never to hands played or chips won. The shop should be small, sectioned and preview-first, with honest rotation dates, no random rewards of any kind, and the only real-money price, Pro, shown in full. Most of the layout evidence is practitioner convention, not published A/B results, so the launch numbers below are starting points to calibrate, not proven optima.

## Players pay for identity, but cosmetics rarely carry the business

The first lesson from the evidence is about expectations. **Duolingo's IAP revenue fell 11%, 23% and 38% year over year across Q1–Q3 2026, to $6.9M**, while subscriptions brought in $270.0M ([Q3 2026 letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001562088/000162828026069474/q3fy26duolingo9-30x26share.htm)). Duolingo's gem-bought owl outfits (Formal Attire at 400 gems, the Champagne Tracksuit at 600, Super Duo at 1,000) were withdrawn in 2022, and nothing replaced them for four years ([Duolingo Wiki](https://duolingo.fandom.com/wiki/Outfits)). Those outfits were visible only to their owner. The 2026 replacement shows on the learner's social avatar, and learners earn the suits by collecting puzzle pieces through lessons. Junior's Toy Hunt, the first event of this kind, was among Duolingo's "biggest experiment wins for getting learners to complete more lessons," and management now hopes collection events will grow purchases "over time" ([Q3 2026 letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001562088/000162828026069474/q3fy26duolingo9-30x26share.htm); [Duolingo Blog](https://blog.duolingo.com/october-2026-update/)). The design takeaway is that **a cosmetic must be seen by other people to matter**. Ace skins and card backs should therefore appear on leaderboards, Friend Quest cards and shareable result cards, not only on the owner's table.

Card and board games show what players engage with. Hearthstone's card backs come from ranked seasons, achievements, events and pre-order bundles. Since 2021 most backs older than a year can be bought for **500 or 600 gold depending on rarity**, and a single back has sold for 500 gold or $3 ([Hearthstone Wiki](https://hearthstone.wiki.gg/wiki/card_back); [Out of Games](https://outof.games/hearthstone/3210-94-card-backs-and-11-hero-skins-in-hearthstone-will-be-purchasable-for-gold-in-patch-204-this-week); [Out of Games](https://outof.games/news/2699-the-thunder-king-shaman-hero-stolen-thunder-card-back-and-thrall-card-back-are-now-available-in-the-hearthstone-shop/)). Microsoft Solitaire Collection sells single backs at **$0.99–1.99 and themed three-back bundles at $2.99**, and folds the Retro bundle into its Premium subscription ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/1103438575)). Chess.com gives board and piece themes to everyone and runs free limited-time "Special Themes" during events ([Chess.com Help](https://support.chess.com/en/articles/8594320-how-do-i-change-my-background-board-and-pieces)). Balatro gave away four waves of collab deck skins while selling 3.5M copies ([PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/roguelike/after-3-500-000-units-sold-and-its-game-awards-wins-balatro-does-a-victory-lap-and-absorbs-8-new-crossovers-in-its-unstoppable-march-to-turn-all-games-into-cute-little-cards-that-get-you-points-and-stuff)). Table felts are almost never sold on their own: PokerStars makes table themes a free setting, while players buy third-party deck packs for $0.99 ([PokerListings](https://www.pokerlistings.com/simple-guide-to-modding-pokerstars-for-free); [Gumroad](https://azazeyl.gumroad.com/l/owxHh)). That third-party market suggests unmet demand for a coordinated felt-and-deck look, which matching **table sets** can fill.

Character skins sit at the top of every ladder. Fortnite priced outfits at **800 / 1,200 / 1,500 / 2,000 V-Bucks** by rarity and emotes at 500–800 ([Sportskeeda](https://www.sportskeeda.com/esports/latest-items-features-added-fortnite-item-shop-updated-v-bucks-prices)). Brawl Stars runs 29 to 299 gems, about a tenfold range ([Mobile Matters](https://mobilematters.gg/brawl-stars/brawl-stars-guide-to-skin-rarities)). Clash Royale's paid pass is sold largely on one exclusive tower skin and one emote per season ([Supercell](https://supercell.com/en/games/clashroyale/blog/news/pass-royale-price-changes-2/)). Naavik lists the motives as self-expression, not looking default, status, aesthetics and social influence, and cites a survey finding that players are **3x more likely to consider spending on cosmetics than on power** ([Naavik](https://naavik.co/deep-dives/selling-cosmetics-part1/)). No public source breaks cosmetic revenue down by category for any card or casino game. The claim that skins outsell backs and emotes rests on price ladders, not sales data.

## A four-step Star ladder prices a Legendary Ace at three weeks of practice

Prices only mean something relative to earn rate. The earlier monetization design pays 10 Stars per lesson, 5 per practice round at 90% or better accuracy, 20 for the daily goal, and fixed streak milestones. Its sinks are a 200-Star Streak Freeze and a 350-Star free-tier Energy refill. The quest rewards proposed below raise a committed learner (five days a week, all quests) to roughly **2,500–3,000 Stars a month**, and a casual learner to about 1,000. Against that, the recommended ladder uses roughly 2.2x steps. That is gentler than Brawl Stars and close to Fortnite's spread, because every item must stay reachable without money.

| Tier | Star price | Committed learner | Casual learner | Typical items |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | Free | Day 1 | Day 1 | 2 card backs, 2 felts, default Ace, base avatar parts |
| Common | 150 | ~2 days | ~5 days | Card-back colorways, avatar frames, chip styles |
| Rare | 400 | ~4 days | ~2 weeks | Illustrated card backs, standard felts, Ace accessories |
| Epic | 900 | ~9 days | ~4 weeks | Premium felts, full Ace outfits, animated card backs |
| Legendary | 1,800 | ~3 weeks | ~2 months | Flagship Ace skins with a custom deal or celebration animation |
| Mastery | Not for sale | Earned by checkpoint | Earned by checkpoint | Graduate card backs, track-completion Ace skins, prestige frames |

The Starter tier follows older store guidance to make the first items earnable within minutes ([Game Developer](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/game-platforms/building-an-in-game-store-for-the-first-time-here-are-the-4-keys-to-success)). Mastery items follow Hearthstone's lesson in reverse. When Blizzard put a formerly exclusive ranked back up for sale, players objected ([Blizzard forums](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/hearthstone/t/why-is-an-exclusive-ranked-card-back-for-sale-now-naxxramas/37716)). Items that prove skill, such as passing the Strategy Master final checkpoint or the Card Counter speed gates, should therefore never be sold for Stars or bundled into Pro. Because most of the Card Counter track is Pro content, its mastery items are indirectly Pro-gated. The free Strategy Master and Smart Player tracks need their own prestige items, so free learners also have something to show off. Rarity here describes production effort, not odds, since nothing is ever drawn at random. Fortnite removed rarity names from its shop in 2024 ([VideoGamer](https://www.videogamer.com/news/fortnite-item-shop-changes-announcement)), so consider showing the tier as a frame color rather than a loud label.

**The catalog should ship as themed sets.** Each set contains one card back, one felt, one Ace skin, one avatar item, and optionally a chip style. Balancy's guidance is to pair one high-demand "main" item with fillers that share its theme, and to keep bundles simple enough that no one needs a calculator ([Game Developer / Balancy](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/production/best-practices-for-in-game-shops-chapter-3-working-on-conversion)). Price each full set at about **20% below the sum of its parts**, shown as plain Star totals ("2,960 → 2,370"). Owning every piece should unlock a fixed, disclosed completion reward: a matching profile frame or deal animation. The evidence on completion rewards is thin. Naavik called PUBG Mobile's set-completion test unproven, and the Liquid & Grit headline claiming skin progressions lift revenue 25% sits behind a paywall ([Naavik](https://naavik.co/f2p-mobile/selling-cosmetics-part-2/); [Liquid & Grit](https://www.liquidandgrit.com/market-coverage/page/3/)). Treat the completion reward as a cheap test, not a known lever.

| Launch set | Card back | Felt | Ace skin | Avatar item | Release path |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Felt & Brass (classic) | Rare | Rare | Epic: tuxedo Ace | Common: bow-tie frame | Always in catalog |
| Midnight Library | Rare | Epic | Legendary: tweed "Professor Ace" | Rare: reading-lamp badge | Always in catalog |
| Neon Grid | Epic (animated) | Epic | Legendary: synthwave Ace | Common: neon frame | Weekly feature rotation |
| Riverboat 1890s | Rare | Rare | Epic: riverboat Ace | Common: pocket-watch badge | Weekly feature rotation |
| Winter Lodge (December event) | Rare | Rare | Epic: knit-scarf Ace | Event badge | Earned through December event; returns for Stars after 12 months |
| Graduate (Strategy Master) | Mastery | — | Mastery: mortarboard Ace | Mastery frame | Final checkpoint only; never sold |
| Pro Atelier | Pro monthly back | Animated felt variant | — | Pro frame | Pro members only (see below) |

Ace needs one extra constraint. Earlier compliance research found that COPPA can reach services that are not aimed at children if they use child-appealing content such as animated characters ([FTC](https://ftc.gov/business-guidance/blog/2025/01/level-tips-businesses-ftcs-settlement-genshin-impact-developer-hoyoverse)), and it recommended avoiding cartoon mascots that draw in children. Ace's skins should look adult and stylish: tailoring, period costume, noir, academic. They should not look like plush toys, costumes for younger children, or candy-colored outfits. No skin should glamorize gambling either. Avoid money-stack, high-roller, or "winning streak" themes.

## Pro should add cosmetic depth, never convert dollars into Stars

The simplest Pro perk would be a Star multiplier, and it is the wrong one. EU consumer authorities' 2025 virtual-currency principles reportedly exempt currency earned only through gameplay ([Reed Smith](https://www.reedsmith.com/articles/qas-on-the-eu-consumer-protection-authorities-joint-guidance-paper/); [Linklaters](https://techinsights.linklaters.com/post/102k6t4/game-changer-eu-introduces-consumer-protection-guidance-for-in-game-virtual-curr)). A paid multiplier effectively turns $9.99 into Stars, which blurs that line. It also makes the "Stars can't be bought" promise false. That conclusion is an inference about soft-law scope, not a legal finding. Pro already gives an honest economic edge: unlimited Energy means Pro members never spend 350 Stars on refills, so more of their earnings go to cosmetics without any multiplier.

Recommended Pro-exclusive items are additive and clearly labeled. They include **one Pro card back each month** that stays claimable by Pro members for 12 months, borrowing Clash Royale's season-exclusive mechanic without its now-or-never pressure ([GINX](https://www.ginx.tv/en/clash-royale-season-17-pass-royale-rewards)). They also include **animated variants** of Epic felts (subtle ambient light), **three saved loadout presets** (a full table look switched in one tap), one extra daily quest reroll, and **7-day early access** to each new set before it goes on sale for Stars. Anything a member has claimed stays owned if they cancel, since Clash Royale's pass items are also "kept permanently once unlocked" ([Supercell](https://supercell.com/en/games/clashroyale/blog/news/season-27-breakdown-rewards-2)). Free users must still be able to reach every Legendary Ace skin and every set with Stars. If the aspirational top of the catalog were Pro-only, it would turn into a paywall on self-expression. Microsoft Solitaire Collection's model, where Premium bundles some backs while the rest stay individually available, supports keeping both routes open ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/1103438575)).

## Three dailies, a forgiving weekly track and one monthly event

The industry has settled on a structure. Hearthstone, Apex Legends, Fortnite (in its Remix season) and Duolingo all use **three daily quests** drawn from a pool. Hearthstone builds the difficulty mix into the draw: it deals one weekly from its 2,500 XP tier and two from its 1,750 XP tier, and it limits rerolls to one per day ([Hearthstone Wiki](https://hearthstone.wiki.gg/wiki/Quests)). Marvel Snap pairs a Normal mission with a Hard one at "double requirements for double rewards," and its weekly challenge is simply "complete N dailies," with milestones at 5/10/15/20/25 ([Out of Cards](https://outof.cards/marvel-snap/guides/272-a-guide-to-daily-missions-in-marvel-snap/); [Marvel Snap Zone](https://marvelsnapzone.com/guides/missions/)). Pokémon GO's seven Field Research stamps **need not be earned on consecutive days, and missing a day resets nothing** ([Bulbapedia](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Research_Breakthrough)). Duolingo boosted rewards for completing all three daily quests in 2025 ([Duolingo Blog](https://blog.duolingo.com/product-highlights/)), awards a monthly badge that can be earned only that month, and weights its social Friends Quest above solo dailies ([Duolingo Blog](https://blog.duolingo.com/friends-quests/)).

| Layer | Structure | Stars | Rules |
|---|---|---|---|
| Daily | 3 quests: Easy, Medium, Hard, drawn at random from a weakness-weighted pool | 5 / 10 / 15, plus a **+15 bonus for all three** (45/day) | Reset at local midnight; unfinished dailies stack for up to 3 days; 1 free reroll per day (Pro: 2); first completion only |
| Weekly track | Complete N dailies out of 21 | 30 at 5, 60 at 10, 100 at 15 | Top tier needs about 70% of dailies, not perfect attendance |
| Weekly quality quests | 2 per week, e.g. "review on 4 different days" | 40 each | 1 free reroll per week |
| Weekly Friend Quest (optional) | Co-op goal with a matched friend over 5 days | 60 each, counts double toward the monthly event | Opt-out in settings |
| Monthly event | Themed chain plus "complete 20 quests this month" | Event badge plus event set pieces | Non-rerollable, one event at a time, threshold well below full attendance |

The ratios follow the observed tiers (Hard at about 2–3x Easy, weeklies at about 2–3x a daily). Still, no public source offers a principled formula for them, so the numbers need calibrating after launch. A reasonable target, which is a design heuristic and not a sourced figure, is **70–85% completion for Easy and Medium and 40–60% for Hard**. Blizzard's 2024 experience is the warning. It made Hearthstone weeklies harder because "many players routinely completed" them, then reverted after backlash, saying it had "pushed too far." Its stated principle was to "reward players for their additional efforts, not make those rewards outside of their reach" ([Blizzard](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24087316/update-on-weekly-quests)). In a learning app, high completion is the point, because the goal is habit.

The quest pool is where this app can do better than games. Volume goals reward persistence, and **"play 200 hands" or "win 5,000 chips" would teach gambling-style persistence and reward luck**. Every quest should instead measure decision quality or spacing. Easy quests can be "finish 1 drill" or "review 5 due cells." Medium quests can be "20 hands at 90%+ accuracy" or "clear your review queue." Hard quests can be "15 correct soft-hand decisions in a row" or "95% on a true-count drill at target speed." Draw quests from each learner's weakest strategy cells and overdue reviews. A secondhand study finds that only adaptive, performance-personalized gamification significantly improved student outcomes ([Springer, J. Comput. Educ. 2025](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s40692-025-00366-x)), though no controlled study compares accuracy-based quests with volume-based ones. Pokémon GO's one-stamp-per-day rule is a useful model: cap quest progress per day so that regular practice beats marathon sessions.

Expect quests to be one useful lever, not a transformative one. No public controlled A/B result isolates the effect of daily quests. Frommel and Mandryk's survey of 178 players found the same mechanics experienced as both motivating and as FoMO-driven chores ([Utrecht University](https://research-portal.uu.nl/en/publications/daily-quests-or-daily-pests-the-benefits-and-pitfalls-of-engageme/)). Duolingo's November 2026 backlash shows how this goes wrong. Several events ran at once, and post-lesson chests took several taps to open. One user said they now log in only weekly, and Duolingo began testing an opt-out from event screens ([Android Authority](https://androidauthority.com/duolingo-event-screen-opt-out-3737299)). The Quests tab should therefore hold rewards for **one-tap claiming at the user's convenience**. Show a single toast after a lesson instead of a chest sequence, run one event at a time, and offer the opt-out from day one.

## A small, preview-first shop that never sells chance

Practitioner guidance converges on a small, sectioned shop: about **5 items (plus or minus 2) in focus per category**, roughly 6 visible without scrolling, and categories added only when needed ([devtodev](https://www.devtodev.com/resources/articles/how-to-improve-a-free-to-play-in-game-store)). Clash Royale's 2023 redesign separated the Shop (Offers, Market, Cosmetics) from a Collection tab for owned and equipped items ([RoyaleAPI](https://royaleapi.com/blog/2023-december-update?lang=en)). Fortnite's 2023 Locker redesign drew enough criticism that Epic publicly acknowledged it, which shows the equip surface can fail even when the shop works ([Sportskeeda](https://www.sportskeeda.com/fortnite/epic-games-working-fix-fortnite-s-locker-ui)). Supercell's minimal, timer-light shops are the trust-building model. That fits an education brand better than casino-style urgency ([Gamigion](https://www.gamigion.com/evolution-of-in%e2%80%91game-shops-in-mobile-f2p-games/)).

| Zone | Content | Behavior |
|---|---|---|
| Sticky header | Stars balance, "Stars are earned, never sold" info link | Always visible on every shop view |
| Featured hero | One full table set rendered on a live table | Tapping opens a set preview with "Buy set" and "Buy pieces" |
| This week | 3–4 rotating items with their real rotation-out date | Weekly, not daily, rotation; date shown on each tile (Fortnite has shown rotation-out dates since 2024) |
| Category chips | Sets · Card Backs · Felts · Ace · Avatar · Pro | Horizontally scrollable; grid of 2 columns on phone, 3 on tablet, 4–6 on desktop |
| Tiles | Item shown in use, name, Star price, one state chip (New / Owned / Equipped / Leaves Dec 7) | Icon plus text, never color alone; unaffordable tiles show "620 / 900" with "Ways to earn" linking to Quests |
| Detail view | Live preview on the user's own table (dealt hand, Ace reacting), "Try for one hand," set progress "2/4" | Short bottom sheet with a Close button and Back support on mobile; side panel on desktop |
| Pro section | Pro perks and the full real-money price | Total billed, period, trial end date and renewal terms next to the button |
| Collection (separate tab) | Owned items, loadout presets, equip | Every "Owned" tile in the shop deep-links here |

**Live preview is the highest-value feature.** Direct game data is not public. E-commerce figures point the same way but are vendor-reported, not controlled: shoppers who viewed a 3D model were 44% more likely to add to cart, and AR viewers were 65% more likely to buy ([Shopify](https://www.shopify.com/blog/ar-shopping)). Baymard finds that visually driven list items without in-context thumbnails tend to be ignored ([Baymard](https://baymard.com/research-articles/current-state-product-list-and-filtering)). Fortnite added an enlarged hover preview panel ([DBLTap](https://dbltap.com/posts/all-fortnite-item-shop-changes-in-update-v26-20-01hb93k31mms)). NN/g warns against stacking bottom sheets or using them in place of full detail pages ([NN/g](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/bottom-sheet/)), so the set preview should open as a full page. Meet WCAG 2.2 targets (24×24 CSS px minimum; 44 pt and 48 dp on the platforms) and 4.5:1 contrast, and check gold-on-green felt palettes in particular ([W3C](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/contrast-minimum.html)).

The guardrails come from enforcement history, not taste. The FTC's $20M HoYoverse settlement targeted confusing multi-step currency and misrepresented loot-box odds ([FTC](https://ftc.gov/business-guidance/blog/2025/01/level-tips-businesses-ftcs-settlement-genshin-impact-developer-hoyoverse)). Epic paid $245M in refunds partly over one-press purchases made without affirmative consent ([PBS](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/nation/fortnite-maker-epic-games-to-settle-ftc-case-pay-520-million-in-penalties-and-refunds)). Both stores require odds disclosure for any paid random item ([Apple Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/); [Google Play](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9858738)). Belgium treats paid loot boxes as gambling, though that claim comes from secondary sources ([Promise Legal](https://blog.promise.legal/lootbox-regulation-2026-game-studios/)). Research also finds that free loot boxes and games with gambling content tend to *precede* monetary gambling ([J. Behav. Addict. 2023](https://doi.org/10.1556/2006.2023.00069)). In a blackjack trainer, the right line is therefore stricter than the law: **no randomness anywhere in rewards, not even free chests**. Every quest, milestone and event reward is fixed and shown in advance.

Scarcity must be honest. Balancy's rules are that "Limited" needs a real counter or end date, discounts are rounded honestly, and "Most Popular" must be true ([Game Developer / Balancy](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/production/best-practices-for-in-game-shops-chapter-3-working-on-conversion)). Hearthstone's pattern, where event backs return later at a fixed earned price, lowers FOMO without cheapening real prestige. Rotating and event items should state "returns to the catalog after 12 months." Only Mastery items say "never sold," and that promise must be kept permanently. Avoid fake countdowns, "only 3 left" on digital goods, and timers that silently reset. Every Star spend needs a confirm step. Because Stars are never sold, the only real-money price in the app is Pro, and it must follow the subscription disclosures and cancellation rules already set: total billed most prominent, trial end date, renewal terms, and cancellation as easy as sign-up. Track five numbers from launch: shop open rate, preview-to-purchase rate, equip rate within 24 hours, quest completion by tier, and accuracy change on quest-targeted cells. The last one is the metric that tells you whether the economy is teaching.

## Conclusion

The research reframes what the shop is for. In this app the catalog is not a store so much as a **visible transcript of practice**. Each Star comes from an accurate decision or a timely review, so an Epic felt is a readable record of about nine days of quality play, and a Graduate Ace is proof of a passed checkpoint. That gives the app something Duolingo's 2022 owl outfits lacked: items that mean something to other learners. It also turns the gambling-adjacent risk into a design asset. Putting rewards on accuracy, spacing and checkpoints, never on volume, outcomes or chance, is at once the ethical choice, the choice that keeps the store review simple, and the one that best fits what the app teaches.

The main open questions are empirical. The test plan should answer whether the 2.2x price steps feel fair at real earn rates, whether set-completion rewards move purchases at all, whether Pro's early access and monthly back measurably lift trial-to-paid conversion, and whether Hard-quest thresholds land near 50% completion. Calibrate prices from observed Star balances after 30 days. Raise or lower prices only on future items, so that no one's saved Stars lose value.
