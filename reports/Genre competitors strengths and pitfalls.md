# Win on trust, proof of skill, reach

The products that last in both genres make money from **repeat engagement, sold honestly**. Duolingo and Chess.com sell subscriptions on top of a habit loop. Blackjack Apprenticeship (BJA) sells **$397–$997 memberships and a $3,499 bootcamp**, fed by a free YouTube channel. Social casinos earn **$200–$280 a month per paying user** from the 4–10% of players who buy coin packs. The failures share a cause: monetization that outran how users felt. Duolingo's energy meter stops even perfect play, and the company now says it is giving up **more than $50M in bookings** to win back free users. Subscription apps lose trust through surprise renewals. Poker trainers blur education with operator ties and "$25M+ student earnings" claims. Social casinos face a **$155M settlement**, a **$24.9M jury verdict**, and a wave of state bans that reach affiliates. Felt & Fortune already avoids most of these traps: it has goal-based tracks, decision-graded trainers, earned-only Stars, no random paid rewards, and honest house-edge messaging. Its gaps sit in three areas. **Trust plumbing** is missing: in-app account deletion, email for receipts and renewal reminders, and terms that cover billing. **Proof of learning** is missing: no skill rating and no review of missed hands. **Reach** is missing: no public free tools, no reminders, no analytics, and an empty community. The roadmap at the end puts trust and compliance first, then skill measurement and acquisition, then credentials and video. The owner's completed requests (random coach reactions, the sound rework, removal of "free" claims) and the deferred thumb-first mobile layout are noted only where they connect to that roadmap.

## Two genres, two money machines

The gamified-learning apps earn from **subscriptions layered on a free habit**. Chess.com gives free members one Game Review a day and reserves unlimited review, explanations and Insights for its Platinum and Diamond tiers ([Chess.com support](https://support.chess.com/en/articles/8609584-september-1st-plan-changes)). Lichess gives away the same analysis with no ads, funded by donations ([Lichess forum](https://lichess.org/forum/lichess-feedback/lichess-vs-chesscom)). That shows a paid product must beat a free rival on polish and progression, not on information. Brilliant and Babbel gate content behind a trial. Speak charges about **$17.99 a month or $83.99 a year** and hides the price until the in-app paywall ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/speak-learn-english/id1286609883)). The education category's medians are **$9.99 a month and $44.99 a year**. Education buyers deliberate: only 28.5% of conversions happen on Day 0, the lowest share of any category ([RevenueCat 2026](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2026-education)). Freemium apps convert a median **2.1%** of users by Day 35 ([RevenueCat](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/subscription-app-trends-benchmarks-2026.md)). Duolingo's ex-CPO credits retention, not acquisition, with its 4.5x growth in daily users: current-user retention had **5x the impact of the next-best lever** ([Lenny's Newsletter](https://lennysnewsletter.com/p/how-duolingo-reignited-user-growth)).

The card-game genre splits three ways. **Social casinos** sell virtual coins with no cash value. Playtika earned **$2.76B in FY2025** while its daily users fell 1.3% and payer conversion rose to 4.5% ([Playtika IR](https://investors.playtika.com/news-releases/news-release-details/playtika-holding-corp-reports-q4-and-2025-financial-results)). DoubleDown's payers spend about $200–$280 a month ([DDI 20-F](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1799567/000162828026022395/ddi-20251231.htm)), and social casino depends on its top 1% of spenders more than any other mobile genre ([SDU whitepaper](https://gdlt.sdu.dk/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/whitepaper_crackingthecode-1.pdf)). The sector is flat. It grows by squeezing payers and moving sales to direct web stores, which made up **37% of Huuuge's Q4 2025 revenue** ([Huuuge IR](https://ir.huuugegames.com/en/news/huuuge-inc-announces-fy-q4-2025-financial-results)). A web-sold subscription like Felt & Fortune's already has that margin. **Sweepstakes casinos** add a redeemable second currency. VGW reported about A$7.3B gross ([Gambling Insider](https://www.gamblinginsider.com/news/162895/chumba-casino-revenue-vgw-7-3bn-going-private)), but that dual-currency structure is exactly what state law now bans. **Skill trainers** charge for depth and verification. GTO Wizard runs from **$49 to $289 a month** ([PokerNews](https://www.pokernews.com/news/2026/03/gto-wizard-subscription-plans-new-features-pricing-50908.htm)). Mobile blackjack apps sit at $5–$10 a month or $40–$90 a year, with TruCount at **$9.99 a month or $79.99 a year** ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6757248479)). BJA's "seven figures" rest on expensive live services and a 450K-subscriber YouTube funnel ([Niche Pursuits](https://www.nichepursuits.com/colin-jones/)).

| Model | Who | How the money comes in | Lesson for Felt & Fortune |
|---|---|---|---|
| Freemium subscription | Duolingo, Chess.com, Brilliant, Babbel, Speak | Free habit, paid depth or unlimited use | Felt & Fortune's model; retention is the lever |

Felt & Fortune's **$9.99 a month** matches the education median and TruCount. Its **$49.99 a year** works out to a 58% discount, well beyond poker's usual 20–30% annual discount ([PokerNews](https://www.pokernews.com/poker-coaching/)). The steep discount is a deliberate push toward annual plans, and it is worth testing rather than changing blindly.

## The winners sell a feedback moment and a visible skill number

Across both genres, the most valuable feature is a **feedback moment right after play**. Chess.com's Game Review is its best paywall because it answers "how did I do, and what did I miss" at the moment a player cares most. Users fought hard when free access was cut, posting complaints such as "you just gutted my way of improving" ([Chess.com forum](https://www.chess.com/forum/view/game-analysis/you-just-gutted-my-way-of-improving-and-put-it-behind-a-paywall-thanks-for-nothing)). Blackjack trainer reviewers praise apps that grade the decision rather than the outcome, and they ask for **an explanation after every mistake** ([App Store, Blackjack Guru](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id6717589612)). BJA's reviewers credit it with closing the gap "between knowing and doing" through live dealing and testing ([CountingEdge](https://www.countingedge.com/blackjack-apprenticeship/)).

The credible products also **prove progress with an external yardstick**. Chess has Elo and per-game accuracy ([Chess.com features](https://www.chess.com/article/view/chesscom-features)). Khan Academy found that skills mastered, not minutes spent, predicted test gains: every 10 extra skills was worth about one point on Newark's state test ([Khan white paper](https://blog.khanacademy.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Newark_White_Paper-2.pdf)). XP and streaks measure engagement. Felt & Fortune's own Profile shows "Level: Counter" to a Smart Player learner because the level comes from placement, not progress. That bug is a symptom: the product has no number that proves a learner is getting better.

The habit layer is well proven. A learner on a 7-day streak is **2.4x more likely to return** the next day ([Duolingo blog](https://blog.duolingo.com/how-duolingo-streak-builds-habit)). Leaderboards lifted learning time **17%** and tripled highly engaged learners ([Lenny's Newsletter](https://lennysnewsletter.com/p/how-duolingo-reignited-user-growth)). The streak-saver notification was Duolingo's first big streak win. Social casinos add the content-neutral metas that survive transfer to education: **event cadence**, which Sensor Tower credits for Monopoly Go's record **$6B** ([Sensor Tower](https://sensortower.com/blog/monopoly-go-app-revenue-milestone)), and repeatable collection albums ([Naavik](https://naavik.co/digest/live-ops-trends-powering-mobile-puzzle/)). Felt & Fortune has streaks, freezes, quests, leagues and sets. It is missing the **reminder** that makes streaks work, and the **friends** that give leagues a social layer.

Acquisition in this niche is creator-led and tool-led, not paid. Chess.com's biggest surges came from PogChamps and the Mittens bot, which drew **8.9M registrations a month** in early 2023 ([PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/honestly-this-sucks-says-chesscom-as-its-servers-cant-keep-up-with-chesss-explosive-popularity)). BJA moved from an ebook to a **free 6-video mini course**, and embedding videos on landing pages roughly doubled click-through ([Niche Pursuits](https://www.nichepursuits.com/colin-jones/)). Wizard of Odds draws about **716K visits a month**, 48% of them direct, the pattern of a bookmarked reference tool ([Semrush](https://sv.semrush.com/website/wizardofodds.com/overview)). GTO Wizard runs a free competitive mode as its top of funnel ([PokerNews](https://www.pokernews.com/news/2025/07/play-pokerarena-free-competitive-1v1-poker-by-gto-wizard-49213.htm)). Shareable recaps work better than bribes. Duolingo's Year in Review spiked new users, while its free-month referral program added only **3%** ([Duolingo blog](https://blog.duolingo.com/year-in-review-behind-the-scenes); [Lenny's Newsletter](https://lennysnewsletter.com/p/how-duolingo-reignited-user-growth)). Felt & Fortune keeps its strategy charts, house-edge tools and count demo behind login, which hides its best acquisition assets.

## They fail when money outruns trust

Duolingo is the cautionary case. Its energy system drains a unit for **every exercise, correct or not**, so skilled learners hit a wall that hearts never imposed. Users called it "the final straw" and started petitions ([Android Authority](https://www.androidauthority.com/quitting-duolingo-energy-system-3599842/)), and one reviewer complained that paying simply made the app easier ([PCWorld](https://www.pcworld.com/article/2924235/duolingo-got-easier-when-i-paid-for-it-that-was-the-problem.html)). Management later tied slowing user growth to the monetization push and guided to giving up more than $50M in bookings to remove free-tier friction ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/duolingo-q4-earnings-call-highlights-041400802.html)). Stacked chest and event screens between lessons drew complaints that they "interfere with learning," and Duolingo is now testing an opt-out ([Android Authority](https://androidauthority.com/duolingo-event-screen-opt-out-3737299)). Felt & Fortune meters only standalone practice with Energy, which is right. It should keep the meter generous, keep earn-back tied to accuracy, and add no screens between a hand and the next decision.

Subscription apps lose trust mostly over **billing, not content**. Brilliant reviewers report surprise renewals and refused refunds. Babbel's recurring complaint is a trial that turned into an annual charge ([Trustpilot: Brilliant](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/brilliant.org?page=6); [Trustpilot: Babbel](https://uk.trustpilot.com/review/babbel.com?page=3)). Blackjack trainer users call developers "greedy" and say they cannot find how to cancel ([JustUseApp](https://justuseapp.com/en/app/6717589612/blackjack-guru-bj21-trainer/reviews)). Upswing's refund guarantee can be refused once a member has "consumed a significant portion" of the content ([Upswing](https://upswingpoker.com/lab/)). Felt & Fortune currently has no email infrastructure, so it cannot send receipts, trial-ending notices or renewal reminders. California and New York require those, and they are the single cheapest way to stand apart from these complaints.

Grading errors are fatal in a domain with exact answers. Speak loses credibility when wrong pronunciations score as correct ([LanguaTalk](https://languatalk.com/blog/speak-app-review/)). A Blackjack Guru reviewer reported a correct double-down marked wrong, and another found no multi-hand, full-table counting practice ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id6717589612)). Felt & Fortune's server-side engine, with its JS and Python parity test, is a real moat, and it should be advertised.

The card-game genre adds legal and ethical failure modes. Washington courts held that purchasable, non-cashable chips are "something of value," which produced the **$155M Big Fish settlement** ([Casino.org](https://www.casino.org/news/big-fish-155-million-settlement-finalized-by-federal-judge/)). California's AB 831 and New York's S5935A ban dual-currency sweepstakes and **extend liability to affiliates and vendors** ([ZwillGen](https://www.zwillgen.com/gaming/californias-ab-831-bans-sweepstakes-casinos-expands-liability-vendors/); [NY Senate](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/bills/2025/S5935A)). Florida's attorney general sued VGW and Stake partly over "free," "100% safe" and "100% legal" marketing ([Casino.org](https://www.casino.org/news/florida-ag-sues-vgw-stake-payment-firms-in-blistering-attack-on-sweepstakes-casinos/)). Removing Felt & Fortune's "free" claims was therefore prudent beyond accuracy. Making micro-transactions was the **only unique predictor** of social casino players migrating to online gambling ([PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25410278/)). Among trainers, the ethics risk is conflict of interest. A sweepstakes poker operator bought Upswing, which leads with "$25M+ Student Earnings" and pays members in sweeps coins ([CardPlayer](https://www.cardplayer.com/poker-news/upswing-poker-doug-polk-sale-clubwpt-gold); [Upswing](https://upswingpoker.com/lab/)). Wizard of Odds is owned by a casino-affiliate network ([Wikipedia](https://www.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Shackleford)). New trainer apps now market "no real-money gambling, no ads" as a selling point ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6780065723)).

## Felt & Fortune is strong on design and thin on plumbing and reach

Measured against this evidence, Felt & Fortune's core product design is ahead of most competitors. Its placement survey and test-outs mirror Upswing's placement quiz. Its checkpoints, which require 90% accuracy on two separate days, follow Khan's mastery model. Its decision-graded trainers answer the top trainer complaint. Its earned-only Stars, absence of randomized paid rewards, and play chips that cannot be purchased stay outside every element that triggered *Kater*, *Larsen* and the sweepstakes bans. Its 145-casino map is a free-tier cousin of BJA's paid Casino411 database.

The audit's problems cluster in four areas. The first is **store and consumer-law gaps**: no in-app account deletion or data export, no email, terms that do not cover billing or refunds, and an About page that still calls the site a student project. The second is **features that cut against the positioning**. The Track tab is a real-money session and profit tracker, which reads as a gambling aid under Apple guideline 5.3.4. The site also runs two overlapping casino directories. The third is **missing proof of learning**: no skill rating, no mistake history, and a level label that contradicts the track. The fourth is **missing growth machinery**: no reminders, no analytics, no public tools, an empty community, no first-run tour, text-only lessons, and unbundled assets that load slowly on mobile. None of these needs new research to fix. They need sequencing.

## Conclusion

The research points to one conclusion. In a gambling-adjacent niche, **trust is the product's real differentiator**, and every competitor that stumbled spent it to buy short-term revenue. Duolingo spent it on friction, Brilliant and Babbel on renewals, Upswing on operator ties, and social casinos on currency sales. Felt & Fortune's economy design already refuses those trades. Its risks now come from what is missing (email, deletion, honest billing terms) and from one legacy feature, the real-money Track tab, that contradicts its own positioning. The biggest product opportunity is to make skill visible: a rating and a hand review turn "I practiced" into "I got better." That number is also the most credible shareable asset and the natural Pro hook.

Three uncertainties remain. No public data shows conversion rates for blackjack or poker trainers, so the funnel must be measured, which is why analytics sits in "Now." No legal opinion directly covers paid blackjack simulators that sell no chips and offer no prizes, so counsel should confirm the CA, NY and WA position before launch. Most user-sentiment evidence comes from self-selected reviews, so it shows what people complain about, not how often.
