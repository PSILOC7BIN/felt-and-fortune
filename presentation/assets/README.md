## Generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: original presentation cover artwork for Felt & Fortune, a free educational blackjack practice and analytics web app. Create a refined 1970s screenprinted editorial still life of three ivory playing cards fanned at a slight angle on deep olive green felt, one clear ace of spades, a mustard yellow card back with tasteful retro concentric curves, subtle burnt-orange accents, soft espresso shadows, tactile paper grain. Sophisticated art-school poster aesthetic, not photoreal, no shiny casino glamour. Wide 16:9 composition with ALL card objects concentrated in the rightmost 45 percent; the left 55 percent is quiet uninterrupted warm cream #F4E6CA paper for editable title text to be placed later. Palette strictly warm cream #F4E6CA, burnt orange #B85B35, mustard #D2A442, olive #69734A, espresso #30251E. No brand logos, no headlines, no extra words, no money, no chips, no people, no watermark. Elegant large negative space, beautiful restrained texture. This is decorative concept artwork, not a screenshot.
