Live hands, flashing cards, the 3D tray, timed true counts, and full mini-shoes, with up to about 25 interactions per lesson.

Every answer comes from one strategy engine, and missed questions come back.

About 45 seconds. Lessons used to be mostly multiple choice. Now each one teaches an idea and then has you use the real tool: live hands, cards flashing past to count, the 3D discard tray, timed true-count conversions, bet sizing, and full mini-shoes that chain it all together. Concept questions got harder too, like hourly standard deviation and risk of ruin. Every answer comes from the same engine as the charts, so a lesson can never disagree with the rest of the site, and anything you miss comes back at the end, like in Duolingo.
