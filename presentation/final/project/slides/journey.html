About 60 seconds. Here is how the project actually unfolded. In mid-September I started by researching existing advantage-play tools, and my first goal was to closely match one of them. That didn’t hold up: its signed-in features weren’t available to study, and a copy wouldn’t teach me much. So on September 21 I pivoted to an original product, Felt & Fortune, with a 1970s editorial look. That’s what I presented in the proposal. Through October and November I built out and cleaned up the toolbox. For example, I found the EV Calculator and the Monte Carlo Simulator were literally the same page, so I merged them.
