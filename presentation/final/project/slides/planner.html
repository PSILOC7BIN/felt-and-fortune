Step 2 · The planner

A transparent, rule-based “AI”

Track + placement

Your goal and proven skill pick one of four tracks. Self-reported skill only counts when the checks agree.

Focus areas

A missed check, or your hardest skill, adds a review lesson to that unit.

Pace

Daily minutes set lessons per day, a daily XP goal, and a finish date.

Context

Your games add notes, e.g., online games reshuffle. Switch tracks any time.

Why rules instead of a large language model? Zero cost, no wrong blackjack math, and every decision can be explained.

About 50 seconds. Here’s the brain of it: an expert system, a set of explicit rules rather than a black box. First it picks one of four tracks from your goal and what you proved in the checks. If you want a real edge but haven’t proven counting, you start one track earlier. Then it places you inside the track, and self-reported skill only counts when the checks agree: claim you count but miss the count check, and you get counting review instead of a skip. A missed check or your hardest skill adds a review lesson, your daily time sets the pace and finish date, and your games add notes. I chose rules over a language model because they cost nothing, can’t get blackjack math confidently wrong, and every decision can be explained.
