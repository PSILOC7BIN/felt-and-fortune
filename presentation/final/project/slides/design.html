About 50 seconds. I redesigned the whole site, not just the new section, as a fusion of two styles. From Apple: the system color palette, system fonts, frosted-glass navigation, segmented controls, and a messages page styled like iMessage. From learning apps like Duolingo: chunky buttons with a pressable bottom edge, big answer tiles, the winding path, streaks, XP, and celebration screens. One lesson learned: several of Apple’s brightest colors don’t have enough contrast for white text, so I used deeper shades for banners and buttons to meet accessibility guidelines. The mascot, the sounds, and the logo are all original. I didn’t reuse any other company’s branding.
