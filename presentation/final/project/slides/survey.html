Step 1 · Onboarding survey

Ten questions, four of them real skill checks

  • Experience, goal, hardest skill, games played, daily time, and learning style
  • Four real checks: hard 16 vs. 10, soft 18 vs. 9, a Hi‑Lo count, and a true count
  • “Not sure yet” is always an option, so beginners don’t have to guess
  • An account is created only after you’ve seen your plan
About 55 seconds. When you click Get started, Ace, the site’s mascot (an original playing-card character I drew for the project), walks you through ten questions, one at a time. Six are about you: how much you’ve played, what you want out of this, what feels hardest, what games you play, how much time you have, and how you like to learn. The other four are real skill checks. This one asks for the best play with 16 against a dealer 10; the answer is hit. Then there’s a soft 18 against a 9, a short Hi‑Lo running count, and a true-count conversion. Every check has a Not sure yet option, so a beginner isn’t forced to guess. And like Duolingo, you see your plan before you’re asked to make an account.
