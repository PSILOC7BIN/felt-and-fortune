The problem

Plenty of blackjack tools. No path through them.

Charts

Tell you the right play, but not how to remember it under pressure.

Calculators

Answer one question at a time, with no sense of what to learn next.

Spreadsheets

Track results, but short-term wins can hide bad decisions.

A beginner can’t tell where to start. An experienced player can’t tell what to skip.

About 55 seconds. Learning blackjack well is a ladder of skills: the rules, basic strategy, then counting cards, converting to a true count, betting with the count, and finally changing your plays based on the count. There are good free resources for each step: strategy charts, calculators, spreadsheets for tracking results. Two existing sites I studied, AP Toolbox and Blackjack.tools, already bundle a lot of this. The problem I kept running into is that none of them tells you where you are on that ladder. A beginner doesn’t know where to start, and someone who already knows basic strategy has to wade through material they’ve already mastered. That gap became the center of my final project.
