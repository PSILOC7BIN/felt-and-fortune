About 45 seconds. This is the decision that shaped the final project. I realized the site needed an intelligent feature [confirm: and that the course expects one], and the toolbox needed a front door. So sign-up now works like a learning app: you answer ten quick multiple-choice questions, a planner figures out where you are, and you get a personal path of short lessons, in the style of Duolingo, taking you from wherever you are to playing like a pro.
