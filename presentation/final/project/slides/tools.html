Step 6 · Practice

Seven tools, one job each

  • Strategy Trainer: the right play, chart built in
  • Count Trainer: cards dealt at speed
  • Deck Estimator: a 3D tray, to the half deck
  • True Count Trainer: timed conversions
  • Index Trainer: count-based plays and insurance
  • Shoe Challenge: everything, three hearts
  • Bankroll Simulator: EV and risk of ruin
About 45 seconds. The old toolbox had thirteen overlapping pages. I cut it to seven tools with one job each, and every one is both a standalone page in Practice and a building block inside lessons. The one I’m proudest of is the Deck Estimator: a 3D discard tray you can drag to rotate, with a one-deck reference beside it, answered to the nearest half deck like a real tray. The Shoe Challenge puts everything together: twelve rounds from a real shuffled six-deck shoe, graded on the server, with three hearts.
