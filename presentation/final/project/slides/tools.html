Step 6 · Practice

Seven tools, one job each

  • Strategy Trainer: the right play, chart built in
  • Count Trainer: cards dealt at speed
  • Deck Estimator: a 3D tray, to the half deck
  • True Count Trainer: timed conversions
  • Index Trainer: count-based plays and insurance
  • Shoe Challenge: everything, three hearts
  • Bankroll Simulator: EV and risk of ruin
About 60 seconds. The old toolbox had thirteen pages that overlapped: a chart page and a basic strategy drill, a checkout and a career mode, a tutorial that the lessons replaced. I cut it to seven tools, each with one job, and every one of them is both a standalone page in Practice and a building block inside the lessons. The one I’m proudest of is the Deck Estimator: it’s a 3D discard tray you can drag to rotate, with a one-deck reference stack beside it, and you answer to the nearest half deck, exactly like reading a real tray. The Shoe Challenge puts everything together: twelve rounds from a real shuffled six-deck shoe, graded on the server, with three hearts and a leaderboard. Practice sessions also count toward your daily XP goal.
