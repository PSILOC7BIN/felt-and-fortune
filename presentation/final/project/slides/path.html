About 60 seconds. This is My Path, its own section of the site. There are four tracks, each with eight units and thirty lessons, a hundred and twenty in all, and each has about four hundred graded questions, so no track is a shortcut. Smart Player covers rules, strategy, payouts, money, and casino know-how. Strategy Master covers every hand and rule set. Card Counter goes from Hi‑Lo to index plays, and Advantage Pro adds full indices, Kelly betting, and team play. Your track sits at the top with a Change track button: you can switch any time, your XP and streak stay, and progress in each track is saved. Lessons unlock in order. Units you tested out of stay open for review. Focus areas get an extra review lesson, like the note on this True Count unit. The sidebar tracks your streak, XP, daily goal, and the last seven days, and keeps your plan one click away. Learn is now the home of the whole app: Practice, Track, Community, and Profile sit beside it as tabs, and each unit’s Practice button opens the matching tool.
