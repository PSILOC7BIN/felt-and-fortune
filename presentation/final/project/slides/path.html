Step 4 · My Path

Four tracks, one path each

4 tracks · 8 units each

30 lessons per track

XP · streaks · daily goal

About 45 seconds. This is My Path. There are four tracks, each with eight units, thirty lessons, and about four hundred graded questions, so no track is a shortcut. Smart Player is rules, money, and casino know-how; Strategy Master is every hand and rule set; Card Counter runs from Hi‑Lo to index plays; Advantage Pro adds Kelly betting and team play. Your track sits at the top with a Change track button, and switching keeps your XP and streak. Lessons unlock in order, tested-out units stay open for review, and each unit’s Practice button opens the matching tool.
