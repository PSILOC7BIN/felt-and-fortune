About 55 seconds. This is My Path, its own section of the site. Units run from Table Basics through Hard Totals, Soft Totals, Pairs, Running Count, True Count, Betting, Index Plays, Bankroll and Risk, Casino Craft, and a final Pro Checkout: eleven units and thirty-three lessons. Lessons unlock in order. Units you tested out of stay open for review. Focus areas get an extra review lesson, like the note on this True Count unit. The sidebar tracks your streak, XP, daily goal, and the last seven days, and keeps your plan one click away. Learn is now the home of the whole app: Practice, Track, Community, and Profile sit beside it as tabs, and each unit’s Practice button opens the matching tool.
