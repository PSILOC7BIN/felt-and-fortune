Step 3 · Your plan

Every decision comes with a reason

The plan shows your level, starting unit, daily goal, finish date, and goal, then a “Why this plan” list in plain language.

Example reason

“You got one strategy check right. You’ll start with basic strategy and move quickly through what you know.”

About 45 seconds. After a short building animation, you get your plan. This learner said they use a strategy chart, but only got one of the two strategy checks right, so they start at Hard Totals rather than skipping ahead. At fifteen minutes a day that’s three lessons and a 45 XP daily goal, finishing around December 4. The part I care most about is the Why this plan list underneath: every rule that fired writes a sentence explaining itself. That makes the AI part transparent. A learner, or a teacher, can see exactly why the plan looks the way it does, and retake the placement if they disagree.
