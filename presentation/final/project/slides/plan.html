Step 3 · Your plan

Every decision comes with a reason

The plan leads with your track, then your level, daily goal, finish date, and goal, and a “Why this plan” list in plain language.

Example reason

“You want to understand the game, so you’re on the Smart Player track: rules, a simple strategy, payouts, money management, and casino know-how.”

About 45 seconds. After a short building animation, you get your plan. The first thing you see is your track. This learner knows the rules and wants to understand the game, so they’re on Smart Player. Someone who wants to count gets Card Counter instead, with a completely different set of thirty lessons, not just a later starting point. At five minutes a day that’s one lesson and a 15 XP daily goal, finishing around December 25. The part I care most about is the Why this plan list underneath: every rule that fired writes a sentence explaining itself. That makes the AI part transparent. A learner, or a teacher, can see exactly why the plan looks the way it does, and retake the placement if they disagree.
